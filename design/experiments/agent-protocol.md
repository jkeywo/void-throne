# Fresh LLM playtest protocol

Use five persistent player roles (GN, GS, JN, JS, A) and an independent facilitator. Give each player only current public rules, its faction brief, personality and specifically delivered observations. Keep unbought vision answers with the facilitator. The archived six-agent transcript is forbidden context for fresh players.

Collect five independent drafts before a purchase. Display exact drafts and current covers. Ask A for a card or NONE, pay, then disclose only that answer. Deliver only A's authored disclosures to other roles. Collect both owners' exact final asset signatures. Scope council exchanges to the two tables and deliver cross-table notes only to actual addressees. Collect final orders after replies. Use the game engine for consequences, never as an oracle handed to players before committing.

After R1 resolve, invite exact provisional articles and at most one pledge per faction. Collect distinct article and owner signatures. Apply only enacted pledges in R2. At the Ceremony expose fixed arithmetic and the exact package, collect five fresh decisions, and preserve refusal. Do not prompt a winning coalition or coerce a saving choice. Debrief all six roles.

Store original role contributions, delivered packets, IDs and timestamps. Link an experiment to explicit hypotheses. A report must identify procedural bounds, defaults, information leaks, rule corrections, changed actions and unresolved questions. Human timing needs a separate test. The browser uses one human for all seats; it is not this six-agent workflow.
