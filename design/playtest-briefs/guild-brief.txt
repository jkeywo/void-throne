# Ductus Guild

SHARED BRIEF | TWO PLAYERS

We can carry your army, your food or our own obligations. The same ship cannot be in three places.

You operate the local passage service. Your navigators learn over decades to interpret the immaterial deep; soldiers cannot substitute for them. The immediate problem is the Narrow's worn physical beacon and the demands placed on its ships, not a spell that repairs the void.

Starting assets | Use
Wayfarer - freight vessel | Offer to Navigation, or complete a private commercial contract.
Lantern - survey tender | Offer to Navigation, or Maintain the beacon independently.

Your shared priorities

Protect your people: keep Lantern's navigation team alive and finish with Beacon 2+.
Protect your institution: secure a formal answer to GUILD-14 and protected Guild navigation rights. Prefer N-G's discretion; N-J trades that discretion for a voice over every Navigation order. N-I recognises the licence but leaves traffic priorities to the crown.
Meet your obligations: two commercial contracts are due, one each round. Record how many you complete. Neither paid work nor safe passage should be taken for granted.
Keep the region viable: aim for Communities 2+.

A petition, not a charity charter

Your navigation rights petition has waited fourteen years. The monopoly concerns expertise, licensing and associated services. The articles in this prototype settle licensed passage and duty of service; wider communications rights remain reserved to a later instrument. Every printed option gives a formal response, but on different terms.

You do not automatically give civilian cargo priority. You may do so because people need help, the region sustains your business, or someone offers an acceptable bargain. A compulsory military service duty is a real concession even when you willingly carry troops today.

At the two tables

Your Navigation delegate bargains over the ships and the navigation instrument. Your Security delegate can secure Guard for a risky private voyage or for Lantern's exposed crew, and can negotiate limits on the army's activity. Write one request for each delegate before separating.

Choices you can make alone

Wayfarer Trade uses your own ship and needs no outsider's permission. Lantern Maintain likewise acts without council approval. Both may leave a council order without its required asset. Trade can succeed while imperial relief fails; it can also expose your ship to attack if protection and beacon preparation are inadequate.

You cannot retrain a lost navigation team during this game. Returning perception in veterans is a possible subject for future research, not a supply of qualified navigators.
