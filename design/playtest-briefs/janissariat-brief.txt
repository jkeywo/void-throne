# Janissariat

SHARED BRIEF | TWO PLAYERS

They ask what our soldiers will do when the fighting ends. We are beginning to ask the same question.

Your conditioning was made for war. Without qualifying fighting it frays, weakening combat power and loosening the habits that made the army a dependable imperial instrument. Soldiers begin questioning and sensing things they were trained to suppress. That may be a future for your people. It is a threat to the institution you currently command.

Starting assets | Use
Spear - the 9th Pale Cohort | Offer to Security, launch an independent Raid, or Stand down. It has local assault craft; an external Campaign needs Guild passage.
Vigil - the Narrow garrison | Offer to Security and possible imperial Warrant, or remain Home. Home is an institutional duty, not a Readiness bonus.

Your shared priorities

Keep a capable force: aim for Readiness 3+, preferably 4, and an intact Spear cohort.
Secure your institution: prefer M-A's autonomous order. M-J buys legitimacy but exposes campaigns to another institution's veto. M-I accepts that the crown can direct you to stand down.
Protect your people: do not casually spend Spear at opening Readiness below 2. Conditioning is not the same thing as the welfare of the soldiers who carry it.
Leave somewhere worth defending: aim for Communities 2+.

Your military choices

A Campaign seeks a named armed remnant position beyond the route. It requires Military or Shared passage. A Raid uses Spear's own local craft to intercept an armed remnant detachment on this side of the crossing. It needs no Guild transport, but the target may not appear. Neither action is an attack on the civilian opposition of Vethis.

Actual successful contact sustains conditioning in this prototype. This deliberately narrows the larger game's deployment/internal-trials rule for the author's requested fighting incentive. No approved internal-trials site is available in this local emergency; its costs and legitimacy are outside this test. Guard, food deliveries and orders that never reach a fight do not qualify.

Authority and loyalty

The Ascendant needs your army and its loyalty while it fights. Low Readiness weakens their ability to use Vigil through an imperial Warrant. You are not instructed to betray the throne if conditioning frays. Your players decide what loyalty means in the resulting settlement.

An independent Raid needs only your faction's signatures. It breaches Interim, M-I or M-J duties; under M-A it is permitted, provided Vigil stays Home. A council Raid is a separate lawful order using offered Spear. Do not assign Spear to both.

Your Navigation delegate secures passage or influences the traffic that might draw a target out. Your Security delegate decides military priorities and constitutional terms. Agree what each is authorised to promise.



Weighted settlement: both teammates agree one faction signature. See the public addendum. Temporary service returns surviving assets after round two; missed trade is an acute opportunity cost, not permanent asset loss. Current assets and route determine Guild weight; Readiness determines army weight and also contributes to Ascendant weight.
