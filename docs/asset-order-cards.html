The Void Throne · asset and order cards

Print at 100% on A4, single-sided. Cut around each joined pair, fold on the dashed centre line, and glue the backs together. Each pair makes one asset, not two. Cut order cards separately. Keep a reserve token with Grey Petrel.

Leave cards at home on their chosen face, or take them to their printed council. No faction decision sheet is needed. Traits and home actions are public; exact operational effects stay with the facilitator.

ASSET CARDS · cut outer line / fold centre line

Guild · asset

Wayfarer

Civilian Transport · Troop Transport

At home: Fulfil Commercial Contract

Fulfil the Guild’s commercial contract. A missed contract is lost for this month; the surviving ship returns. An unsafe or exposed passage can halt the voyage or cost you the ship.

At council: offer service

Take to Navigation and Supply. Match these traits to an order. Ignore the home face while at council. One use this round; unused means no home action.

Same home action on both faces. Return surviving cards after resolution.
