The Void Throne

Facilitator Guide, draft v0.4

Your task

Run the two-month crisis and make the consequences legible. Players decide what to offer, which orders to issue, and what government they will accept. Apply the printed outcomes. Do not invent a prophecy, a resource, another player's agreement, an extra veto or a reward for cooperation.

[WORKING] This guide implements multiple funded orders, single-use assets, next-round vetoes, contributor vetoes under Joint authority, Standing Commitments and weighted settlement. The model checks these paper rules; archived simulations retain their earlier rules.

Setup and opening brief

Materials

  • One player rulebook, one qualitative Component Reference, this guide and three shared-role briefs. The Ascendant brief explains foresight and the Warrant; Grey Petrel is an asset in the Component Reference. Keep numerical resolution rules and exact prophecy answers behind the screen.
  • Two council reference sheets and five asset cards: Wayfarer, Lantern, Spear, Vigil and Grey Petrel.
  • Six Charter cards: three Navigation articles and three Military articles.
  • Four vision covers, four qualitative prophecy handouts and four exact facilitator answers: Navigation and Supply, and Security and Government for each round. Covers name councils only. Give the chosen handout to the Ascendant after payment; keep numerical thresholds and modifiers in this guide.
  • Three tracks numbered 0–4, with one counter each; three foresight tokens; one reserve token for Grey Petrel.
  • Four faction sheets and four council sheets: one for each faction and council in each round.
  • Two Ascendant worksheets, one provisional-government proposal and outcome record, two final-package ballot sheets, and a public record for results and contracts.

Blank paper can replace a decision sheet if it records the same choices and outcomes. Use the current vision and settlement rules with any older printed components.

Arrange the table

Place Navigation and Supply at one end and Security and Government at the other. Keep the tracks, current articles and final decisions in a central space where everyone can read them. Provide separate places for the Guild and Janissariat to confer during the faction phase. The Ascendant begins apart from both factions, with the foresight covers.

Seat GN and JN at Navigation and Supply; GS, JS and A at Security and Government. Give both members of each faction their shared brief. Keep the four private vision answers behind your screen; put both current-round covers face up at the start of faction discussion.

Starting position

Give Wayfarer and Lantern to the Guild, and Spear and Vigil to the Janissariat. All four assets begin available. Give the Ascendant three foresight tokens and Grey Petrel's reserve token. The facilitator keeps the vision answers concealed.

Set Beacon to 2, Stability to 3 and Readiness to 3. No contracts are completed. Both articles start Interim and round one has no vetoes. The Ascendant holds the next-round veto in each domain, while factions allocate their assets to any funded legal orders.

The starting settlement weights are Guild 3, Janissariat 3 and Ascendant 6. The Guild has one for each surviving asset and one per two Beacon. If those values remained unchanged, eight of the total twelve weight would be required to pass a settlement.

Prepare the decision sheets

The faction sheet records the round and one agreed assignment for each asset. Both teammates confirm their choices aloud; no signatures are needed. Representatives take their faction's approach to the councils themselves, without written instructions or requests.

The council sheet lists every selected order and the single asset allocated to it. GN records Navigation and JS records Security after council discussion. The Ascendant records only their Warrant at Security; they do not select Navigation orders. Collect both programmes before revealing them. No cross-table messages or operational signatures are needed. Keep a separate public veto strip for each council, with next round, order, issuing party and expiry.

The public record shows the three tracks, available assets, completed contracts, remaining tokens, current articles and binding commitments. Faction drafts and current plans stay with their faction; do not announce them before the Ascendant's choice. Publish final assignments when the faction phase ends. The Ascendant learns current plans through visits after choosing, not through a compulsory statement of intentions.

Opening brief

Allow eight minutes for the briefing. Explain the factions, the two councils and the crisis before taking the players through a round.

The crisis concerns Sorenne's Fall, the southern star system of forty million people described in the lore, and the Sorenne Flag, House Sorenne's surviving fleet. Stability measures the system's supplies and functioning services, not its loyalty. The Flag also makes irregular supply deliveries there; military disruption can therefore harm the people the Dominion is trying to govern. For this scenario, their supply route uses the Narrow, Spear seeks passage to a Flag anchorage beyond it, and a Flag patrol supplies the local Raid target. Those deployments are scenario additions. Players remain at the Seam; each round represents one month.

Run the clock

0–8: briefing. 8–13: round-one faction discussion. 13–18: councils and resolution. 18–23: discuss the result and agree provisional articles. 23–28: round-two faction discussion. 28–33: councils and resolution. 33–38: free discussion. 38–48: Ceremony. Use the remaining twelve minutes for the debrief. These five-minute discussion phases follow the current player rulebook.

The faction phase has one five-minute deadline. Guild and Janissariat players talk only to their own teammates and cannot send messages to the other faction. They begin immediately and budget their time themselves. The Ascendant first chooses one prophecy or NONE privately, using the public state and earlier discussions, before visiting either faction or receiving its current plans. Do not call minute-two or minute-three gates or require factions to announce intentions.

Record the Ascendant's choice before allowing visits. Pay before dealing the exact answer; publish expenditure and remaining foresight, not the chosen council. A choice completes that round's opportunity: no swaps or second purchase. NONE also permits visits. Record NONE if no single valid choice arrives before the phase ends. After choosing, the Ascendant may visit either faction, revisit it, relay offers and share the vision. Do not combine the factions into a table discussion. Unlocked assignments and Grey Petrel's task remain revisable until the faction deadline; the clock does not restart.

At the faction bell, confirm matching faction assignments or apply defaults and then Standing Commitments. Mark unavailable assets absent and record Grey Petrel's assignment. At the council bell, check selected orders against current vetoes and single-use assets; collect both programmes and the Warrant before revealing either. Keep GN and JN at Navigation and GS, JS and A at Security. Resolve the mechanical result during the Free Phase, then collect next-round vetoes under the existing articles before enacting new articles. The five-minute phase does not restart for these recording steps.

In the browser, record the Ascendant's foresight choice first. Faction chat is available from the start; visits open only after the choice has been recorded. The two factions have separate visit conversations with the Ascendant. Final commitments end the same faction phase. An older saved session may contain draft records; keep those drafts within the faction and do not use them to brief the Ascendant.

Resolve in this order

Facilitator only. Players know ownership, assignments, requirements, vetoes, duties and public state. Keep the following numerical effects and concealed scenario conditions behind the screen. Explain observed events without announcing formulas.

Prepare the worksheet

Record opening Beacon, Stability and Readiness, available assets, completed contracts, reserve and foresight, articles, vetoes and Standing Commitments. Copy faction assignments, each selected council order with its asset, and the Warrant. Start Stability change at −1 for monthly consumption. Keep separate entries for prepared Beacon, transport wear, private commercial wear, protection, delivered relief, delivered troops and qualifying combat.

1. Check assignments and order requirements

Each faction confirms matching assignments. Missing agreement uses defaults: Wayfarer Fulfil Commercial Contract, Lantern Maintain the Narrows, Spear Stand Down, Vigil Defend Army Bases. A Standing Commitment overrides a conflicting choice or default. Lost assets are unavailable. The Ascendant assigns Grey Petrel Offer Council Service, Protect the Navigators or Retain the Cutter; missing choice means retention.

Each asset and each printed order can be used once per round. GN allocates offered Navigation assets, including Grey Petrel; JS allocates offered army assets. No authority holder approves these orders. Check each against current vetoes. A veto bars its independent equivalent too: Intercept a Patrol cannot evade a veto through a faction assignment, and a Warrant cannot evade a Protect the Passage veto.

An invalid, unfunded or vetoed order is not attempted and takes no failure penalty merely for being invalid. Resolve double allocations with the owner before the deadline. If they remain unresolved, remove all orders competing for that asset; retain other valid orders. The browser rejects a conflicting submission so the owner can correct it; a missing valid final programme means no council orders. A funded operation may still fail later and pay its printed consequence.

2. Resolve the Warrant and passage protection

A Warrant under Interim or MI needs opening Readiness at least 3 and offered Vigil. If Protect the Passage is vetoed, no Warrant operation is attempted. Otherwise an effective Warrant marks Vigil used, gives protected status and Stability +1. A Warrant under Interim or MI lacking Readiness or offered Vigil costs Stability −1. Under MA or MJ it is advice only, with no effect or penalty.

Protect the Passage with offered, unused Vigil gives protected status and Stability +1. If a valid Warrant already used Vigil, remove the duplicate council order without another penalty. Protection is not combat and does not repair the route. Other orders using separate assets still proceed.

3. Fund Navigation orders

Deliver Relief needs one offered Wayfarer, Lantern or Grey Petrel. Transport Troops needs one offered Wayfarer or Grey Petrel. Repair the Narrows needs offered Lantern. Allocate different ships to simultaneous orders. There is no Shared order and no automatic substitute ship.

Grey Petrel must have been assigned Offer Council Service. If allocated to either transport order, spend its reserve token now, even if the route later halts it. If offered but unused, retain the token. Protect the Navigators cannot also provide transport. A spent cutter is unavailable.

4. Prepare the route

Prepared Beacon equals opening Beacon, plus 1 for available Lantern on Maintain the Narrows, plus 2 for a funded Repair the Narrows. Cap at 4. Lantern cannot perform both tasks or carry relief at the same time. All voyages use this prepared value; deduct wear only after resolving the round.

5. Resolve both transport orders

At prepared Beacon below 2, each funded transport order is halted and costs Stability −1. Neither delivers its cargo. At 2 or more, Deliver Relief adds Stability +2 and delivers civilian relief; Transport Troops provides the passage needed for Attack the Anchorage without a Stability bonus. Resolve each selected order once.

If either or both funded transport orders were selected, mark exactly one transport wear for the round. This is a scheduling cost even if a funded voyage is halted. An invalid or vetoed order is not selected for this purpose. Grey Petrel transport follows the same rule. Two transports do not double this wear.

6. Resolve the commercial contract

Wayfarer on Fulfil Commercial Contract completes at prepared Beacon 3–4, or at 2 with protection: add one contract and one separate commercial wear. At prepared Beacon 2 without protection, disable Wayfarer with no contract and one commercial wear. Below 2 it remains intact in port, with no contract or commercial wear.

Commercial wear is additional to the single council transport wear. Wayfarer cannot complete a contract while offered to council service, even if unused. Lantern and Grey Petrel cannot complete this contract instead.

7. Apply service duties

Each faction takes at most one Stability −1 duty penalty in a round; lost assets and vetoes do not waive duties. Guild under Interim owes no service. Under NG, Lantern must Maintain the Narrows or carry out funded Repair the Narrows. Under NI or NJ, Wayfarer must Offer Council Service. Offering Grey Petrel does not substitute for Wayfarer.

Under Interim, an actual independent Intercept a Patrol assignment breaks Janissariat discipline. Under MI or MJ, Spear must Offer Council Service. Under MA, Vigil must Defend Army Bases. A vetoed independent interception does not launch and therefore does not itself break Interim discipline; other assignment duties still apply.

8. Resolve army operations

Attack the Anchorage uses offered Spear. Without delivered Transport Troops, it cannot leave: Stability −1, no combat, Spear intact. With passage it still costs Stability −1. At opening Readiness 2 or more it achieves qualifying combat. At 0–1 disable Spear with no qualifying combat.

Intercept a Patrol uses Spear, either offered to the council or independently assigned, never both. Every actual interception costs Stability −1. In round one its target is present only if Wayfarer's commercial contract completed. In round two its target is present only if Deliver Relief was delivered, by any of the three eligible vessels. Simultaneous Transport Troops does not cancel that condition.

If no target is present, Spear returns intact without combat. If present at opening Readiness 2 or more, add another Stability −1 and record qualifying combat. If present at Readiness 0–1, disable Spear; add no further Stability loss and no qualifying combat. The target never starts an interception by itself.

9. Apply scenario conditions

Round one: if Lantern delivers civilian relief without protection, lose Lantern's navigation team. Grey Petrel assigned Protect the Navigators prevents this loss and spends its token. Protection prevents it without calling on the cutter. No delivered Lantern relief means no crew-loss trigger. Wayfarer and Grey Petrel relief do not trigger this specific threat.

Round two: delivered Transport Troops without protection costs an additional Stability −1, even if civilian relief also arrived. Protect the Navigators cannot prevent this stores loss. A funded Repair the Narrows combined with a completed Wayfarer commercial contract gives Stability +1. Grey Petrel can carry troops while the other two Guild assets repair and trade, so both conditions can occur together.

Apply these conditions whether a prophecy was purchased or not. Qualitative handouts reveal the same conditional events without the arithmetic. Foresight cannot lift a public veto, restore an asset or guarantee that a player chooses an available order.

10. Finish tracks and assets

Readiness changes once: +1 if any operation achieved qualifying combat, otherwise −1. Protection, routine upkeep, defending bases and unsuccessful expeditions do not provide combat. Final Beacon equals prepared Beacon minus the single transport wear and any commercial wear. Final Stability equals opening Stability plus the complete change ledger. Clamp each final track to 0–4 after adding all effects.

Record losses, contracts and triggered reserve use. Assigned actions use the opening availability; new losses prevent use in later rounds. Vigil stays intact. Surviving faction assets return; Grey Petrel follows its one-service contract. Expire Standing Commitments after round-two operations. Nothing here restores a lost asset.

11. Announce the result

Publish both programmes, the new track values and descriptions, losses, completed contracts and reserve expenditure. Describe what occurred in the fiction. Keep the numerical worksheet and unpurchased conditions behind the screen. Update settlement weights using the public rulebook.

12. Record vetoes for the following round

After the first result, use the existing articles, before enacting replacements. Interim or NI grants A one Navigation veto; NG grants GN one. Interim or MI grants A one Security veto; MA grants JS one. Each may name one order or None. There is no approval gate over the other party's choice.

For NJ or MJ, identify contributing parties from this round's final assignments: any available faction asset on Offer Council Service qualifies its faction; Grey Petrel on Offer Council Service qualifies A. Count offered service even if unused or subsequently lost. Maintenance, private contracts, independent interceptions, defending bases and navigator rescue do not qualify. Each contributing party gets one independent veto in each Joint council, exercised by its delegate there or A. Do not count one per player or asset.

Collect the choices before revealing them together. Missing choice means None; multiple choices on one party's line mean None unless corrected by the deadline. Apply every valid veto. Duplicate names block the order only once; different names can block several or all orders. Record issuer and expiry after the following round. New articles do not remove these vetoes or grant another set. No operational round follows round two, so skip new vetoes then.

Worked resolution

Begin round one at Beacon 2, Stability 3 and Readiness 3. Offer all four faction assets. Navigation allocates Lantern to Deliver Relief and Wayfarer to Transport Troops. Security allocates Spear to Attack the Anchorage and Vigil to Protect the Passage. The Ascendant retains Grey Petrel and issues no Warrant.

Protection adds Stability +1. Both transports are funded; Lantern cannot also maintain. Prepared Beacon remains 2. Relief adds Stability +2; troop transport supplies the attack. Together they produce one transport wear. The attack costs Stability −1 and sustains combat. Protection prevents Lantern's crew loss. Consumption costs Stability −1, giving total Stability change +1. Finish at Beacon 1, Stability 4, Readiness 4, with all assets intact, no contract and Grey Petrel retained.

This cooperation still costs the Guild a contract and a month of route upkeep. Next-round vetoes and constitutional duties remain political choices. From the same starting state, spending Grey Petrel on relief instead allows Lantern to repair while Wayfarer transports troops: the empire gains upkeep, but the Ascendant gives up its sole contracted service.

Fixed visions: facilitator-only answers

These conditions apply whether anyone buys the vision or not. Only give the chosen qualitative handout to the Ascendant. The cover names councils, not specific choices. Existing vetoes and losses may restrict what can change; there is no refund.

R1-N — The exposed transfer

If Lantern delivers civilian relief without passage protection: Lose Lantern. If available Grey Petrel is assigned Protect the Navigators, spend its reserve instead and preserve Lantern.

If Lantern delivers civilian relief with passage protection: No crew loss. A funded Protect the Passage order or effective Warrant prevents it; the rescue reserve is not triggered.

If Lantern does not deliver civilian relief: No crew-loss trigger. Funded but halted transport still pays its normal failure consequence and the round’s single transport wear.

Lantern must be offered to Navigation and allocated to Deliver Relief. Prepared Beacon 2+ delivers the voyage. Protection prevents the loss; navigator rescue prevents only this specific loss. Protection does not repair Beacon.

R1-S — The merchant’s wake

If Wayfarer completes its commercial contract and Spear intercepts a patrol: The interception finds its target. At opening Readiness 2+, it achieves combat at total Stability −2. At Readiness 0–1, disable Spear: Stability −1 and no qualifying combat.

If No commercial contract completes and Spear intercepts a patrol: The interception costs Stability −1, but there is no combat and Spear remains intact. Neither council transport substitutes for a completed private contract.

If Spear does not intercept a patrol: No interception effects. Resolve any attack on the anchorage separately.

A commercial assignment is not enough: the contract must complete. With no qualifying combat, Readiness falls by 1 once. Consumption and duty breaches are additional.

R2-N — Stores at the crossing

If Troop transport arrives without passage protection: Delivered Transport Troops without funded Protect the Passage or an effective Warrant adds Stability −1. This also applies when civilian relief arrives in the same round.

If The Narrows are repaired and Wayfarer completes its commercial contract: Funded Repair the Narrows and a completed private Wayfarer contract add Stability +1. Routine maintenance does not qualify.

If Neither condition occurs: Neither extra Stability effect occurs. Resolve all other effects normally.

Repair adds 2 to prepared Beacon, capped at 4, before voyages. Both conditions can occur: Grey Petrel may carry troops while Lantern repairs and Wayfarer trades. Protection removes the stores loss without removing the distribution benefit.

R2-S — The relief column

If Civilian relief arrives and Spear intercepts a patrol: The interception finds its target after delivered Deliver Relief from Wayfarer, Lantern or Grey Petrel. At opening Readiness 2+, it achieves combat at total Stability −2. At 0–1, disable Spear: Stability −1 and no qualifying combat.

If Civilian relief does not arrive and Spear intercepts a patrol: No target: Stability −1, no qualifying combat and Spear remains intact. Troop transport and private trade do not qualify.

If Spear does not intercept a patrol: No interception effects. A vetoed interception does not launch and incurs no interception cost.

Delivered civilian relief qualifies even if Transport Troops also succeeds. A standing interception veto bars both the council and independent versions. Readiness changes once after all operations.

Trial and final votes

The round-one trial needs spoken agreement from GN+JN+A for Navigation and GS+JS+A for Military. Read back each exact article and its attached terms, independently of the other domain, and record whether it passes. Up to one asset commitment per faction may be attached, with both owners' spoken agreement. Do not quietly detach an unapproved promise to pass its article. No pledge is compulsory. A passed pledge binds only that asset assignment in round two; an informal public plan has no mechanical penalty.

At the Ceremony freeze actual weights. Count both owners’ votes to produce each faction signature; refusal, missing approval or disagreement contributes no support, but the faction’s weight remains in the total. Threshold is ceil(2 × total / 3); at least two signing parties must have positive weight. A zero electorate cannot pass. Count no contracts, verbal concessions or invented influence.

Show the exact final package with five rows, one for each player, and two boxes per row: Assent and Do not assent. Each player ticks one box. A blank row or both boxes ticked gives no assent. If the package fails, permit a replacement within the deadline and use fresh boxes for all five players. Stop on success. If neither of the two permitted packages passes, both provisional articles expire to Interim; no physical state changes. Record dissent even where the weighted package passes. Passing the arithmetic does not establish political consensus.

Checks and debrief

Check cooperation with separate ships, a vetoed order, two orders competing for one asset, a halted double transport, averted Lantern loss, and several Joint contributor vetoes. Check that Grey Petrel cannot transport and rescue in the same month. Compare a missed contract with a permanently lost asset. Failed ratification expires the articles without repairing anything.

Ask for starting and ending constitutional preferences and the event that changed them. Ask each player which teammate contribution mattered. For foresight, distinguish assignment changes, changes in the approach to councils, final order changes, bargaining conditions and confidence. A remaining third token is unavoidable if there were two purchases; do not code it as deliberate saving.

The migrated six-agent run found a useful first purchase and a mostly confirmatory second purchase. It did not show deliberate saving or human timing. Do not guide the next Ascendant to repeat that result. Preserve choices before interpreting them.

Prophecy handouts for the Ascendant

These conditions apply whether anyone buys the vision or not. Only give the chosen qualitative handout to the Ascendant. The cover names councils, not specific choices. Existing vetoes and losses may restrict what can change; there is no refund.

R1-N — The exposed transfer

If Lantern delivers civilian relief without passage protection: Lantern's navigation team is lost. Grey Petrel assigned Protect the Navigators can save the team, spending its contracted service.

If Lantern delivers civilian relief with passage protection: The navigation team survives this threat. Grey Petrel is not called on for rescue.

If Lantern does not deliver civilian relief: This threat does not take the navigation team. Other operations and risks still apply.

You can seek protection, change which vessel carries relief, repair instead, or arrange navigator rescue while those choices remain open.

R1-S — The merchant’s wake

If Wayfarer completes its commercial contract and Spear intercepts a patrol: The expedition finds the Sorenne patrol. A capable force fights and sustains its conditioning, at a further cost to civilian life. A force too weakened to fight is disabled instead.

If No commercial contract completes and Spear intercepts a patrol: The patrol is not found. The expedition disrupts civilian traffic without sustaining the cohort through combat. Council cargo does not substitute for the commercial voyage.

If Spear does not intercept a patrol: These interception consequences do not occur. An attack on the anchorage and other operations have their own consequences.

The Guild can change its vessel assignment before the faction bell. Security can choose an attack instead of an interception if Spear was offered and troop transport is available.

R2-N — Stores at the crossing

If Troop transport arrives without passage protection: Stores are lost during loading, worsening civilian shortages. Protection prevents this extra loss; navigator rescue cannot protect the stores.

If The Narrows are repaired and Wayfarer completes its commercial contract: The combination improves distribution to Sorenne’s Fall. Both operations must happen. Routine maintenance is not the council repair operation.

If Neither condition occurs: Neither additional supply effect occurs. The ordinary consequences of other operations still apply.

Seek passage protection or a different allocation of the vessels. Civilian relief does not cancel the loss of military stores. These two conditions can both occur.

R2-S — The relief column

If Civilian relief arrives and Spear intercepts a patrol: The relief traffic brings the Sorenne patrol within reach. A capable force fights and sustains its conditioning, disrupting civilian life further. A force too weakened to fight is disabled instead. Any of the three relief vessels can draw the patrol.

If Civilian relief does not arrive and Spear intercepts a patrol: The patrol is not found. The expedition disrupts traffic without achieving combat. Troop transport and a private commercial voyage do not draw this patrol.

If Spear does not intercept a patrol: Relief alone causes no attack. Spear must launch an interception for these consequences to occur.

Consider whether Navigation will deliver civilian relief and whether Spear will intercept the patrol. Existing vetoes and commitments restrict choices; they do not compel an otherwise legal order.
