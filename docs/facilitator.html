The Void Throne — Facilitator Guide, draft v0.3

Your task

Run the two-month crisis and make the consequences legible. Players decide what to offer, which orders to issue, and what government they will accept. Apply the printed outcomes. Do not invent a prophecy, a resource, another player's agreement, an extra veto or a reward for cooperation.

[WORKING] This guide implements the revised visions, Trial A Standing Commitments and weighted final settlement. The computer model is an implementation to check, not permission to overrule the paper rules. The browser is an all-seat facilitator test bench; the preserved LLM run is separate evidence.

Setup and opening brief

Materials

  • One player rulebook, one qualitative Component Reference, this facilitator guide and three shared-role briefs. The Ascendant’s brief explains how to choose foresight, issue a Warrant and lodge the reserve. Give councils the player action and Charter descriptions; keep this guide, the resolution worksheet and exact vision answers behind the screen.
  • Two council reference sheets and four asset cards: Wayfarer, Lantern, Spear and Vigil.
  • Six Charter cards: three Navigation articles and three Military articles.
  • Four vision covers, four qualitative prophecy handouts and four exact facilitator answers: Navigation and Supply, and Security and Government for each round. Covers name councils only. Give the chosen handout to the Ascendant after payment; keep numerical thresholds and modifiers in this guide.
  • Three tracks numbered 0–4, with one counter each; three foresight tokens; one reserve token for Grey Petrel.
  • Four faction sheets and four council sheets: one for each faction and council in each round.
  • Two Ascendant worksheets, one provisional-government proposal and outcome record, two final-package ballot sheets, and a public record for results and contracts.

Blank paper can replace a decision sheet if it records the same choices and outcomes. Use the current vision and settlement rules with any older printed components.

Arrange the table

Place Navigation and Supply at one end and Security and Government at the other. Keep the tracks, current articles and final decisions in a central space where everyone can read them. Provide separate places for the Guild and Janissariat to confer during the faction phase. The Ascendant begins apart from both factions, with the foresight covers.

Seat GN and JN at Navigation and Supply; GS, JS and A at Security and Government. Give both members of each faction their shared brief. Keep the four private vision answers behind your screen; put both current-round covers face up at the start of faction discussion.

Starting position

Give Wayfarer and Lantern to the Guild, and Spear and Vigil to the Janissariat. All four assets begin available. Give the Ascendant three foresight tokens and Grey Petrel's reserve token. The facilitator keeps the vision answers concealed.

Set Beacon to 2, Sorenne's Fall's Stability to 3 and Readiness to 3. The Guild has completed no contracts. Write Interim in both article spaces: the Ascendant chooses both council orders, while the factions control their own assets.

The starting settlement weights are Guild 4, Janissariat 3 and Ascendant 6. If those values remained unchanged, nine of the total thirteen weight would be required to pass a settlement.

Prepare the decision sheets

The faction sheet records the round and one agreed assignment for each asset. Both teammates confirm their choices aloud; no signatures are needed. Representatives take their faction's approach to the councils themselves, without written instructions or requests.

The council sheet records the round, current article and final order. Check spoken agreement from whoever holds authority under that article; do not collect initials. The Ascendant stays at Security and Government. If they hold Navigation authority, collect their final Navigation order there at the council deadline and record it for Navigation. Collect both councils' orders before revealing either. Do not relay proposals, requests or replies between tables. Record the Ascendant's foresight choice, reserve task and final Warrant on their worksheet.

The public record shows the three tracks, available assets, completed contracts, remaining tokens, current articles and binding commitments. Faction drafts and current plans stay with their faction; do not announce them before the Ascendant's choice. Publish final assignments when the faction phase ends. The Ascendant learns current plans through visits after choosing, not through a compulsory statement of intentions.

Opening brief

Allow eight minutes for the briefing. Explain the factions, the two councils and the crisis before taking the players through a round.

The crisis concerns Sorenne's Fall, the southern star system of forty million people described in the lore, and the Sorenne Flag, House Sorenne's surviving fleet. Stability measures the system's supplies and functioning services, not its loyalty. The Flag also makes irregular supply deliveries there; military disruption can therefore harm the people the Dominion is trying to govern. For this scenario, their supply route uses the Narrow, Spear seeks passage to a Flag anchorage beyond it, and a Flag patrol supplies the local Raid target. Those deployments are scenario additions. Players remain at the Seam; each round represents one month.

Run the clock

0–8: briefing. 8–13: round-one faction discussion. 13–18: councils and resolution. 18–23: discuss the result and agree provisional articles. 23–28: round-two faction discussion. 28–33: councils and resolution. 33–38: free discussion. 38–48: Ceremony. Use the remaining twelve minutes for the debrief. These five-minute discussion phases follow the current player rulebook.

The faction phase has one five-minute deadline. Guild and Janissariat players talk only to their own teammates and cannot send messages to the other faction. They begin immediately and budget their time themselves. The Ascendant first chooses one prophecy or NONE privately, using the public state and earlier discussions, before visiting either faction or receiving its current plans. Do not call minute-two or minute-three gates or require factions to announce intentions.

Record the Ascendant's choice before allowing visits. Pay before dealing the exact answer; publish expenditure and remaining foresight, not the chosen council. A choice completes that round's opportunity: no swaps or second purchase. NONE also permits visits. Record NONE if no single valid choice arrives before the phase ends. After choosing, the Ascendant may visit either faction, revisit it, relay offers and share the vision. Do not combine the factions into a table discussion. Unlocked assignments and Grey Petrel's task remain revisable until the faction deadline; the clock does not restart.

At the faction bell, ask both faction members to confirm the same assignments. Record one final faction sheet. If either has not confirmed or they disagree, apply the faction defaults; an activated Standing Commitment still overrides them. Mark unavailable assets Absent and record one reserve task. At the council bell, check the current authority and ask the required decision-makers to confirm the final order; also confirm the Warrant. Resolve the recorded decisions. No signatures or initials are needed. During the council phase, keep GN and JN at Navigation, and GS, JS and A at Security. Players may speak only with their council; no visits, calling across, teammate consultations or messages between councils. Rules questions may come to the facilitator, but do not use that route to coordinate decisions. Cross-council plans must be made in earlier phases.

In the browser, record the Ascendant's foresight choice first. Faction chat is available from the start; visits open only after the choice has been recorded. The two factions have separate visit conversations with the Ascendant. Final commitments end the same faction phase. An older saved session may contain draft records; keep those drafts within the faction and do not use them to brief the Ascendant.

Resolve in this order

Facilitator only. Players choose from qualitative asset and order descriptions. Keep the numerical effects, thresholds, preparation order and concealed scenario conditions behind your screen. This changes what players know, not the underlying outcomes.

Players still know ownership, available assignments, assets needed for each operation, Charter authority and duties, public track values and descriptions, foresight costs and final settlement weights. Explain those openly. Do not give a numerical preview of an operation or confirm a hidden condition for free. If asked, explain its stated purpose and known risks; use the printed rules to resolve it.

Prepare a resolution worksheet

Record the round; opening Beacon, Stability and Readiness; available assets; foresight and Grey Petrel tokens; completed contracts; current articles; and binding Standing Commitments. Copy the final assignments, the two council orders and the Warrant choice. Use opening Readiness throughout, not a value adjusted midway through resolution.

Keep separate working entries for Stability change, crossing wear, prepared Beacon, guarded status, delivered council passage, completed Trade and qualifying combat. Start Stability change at −1 for monthly consumption; wear at 0; guarded, delivered, Trade and combat at No. Do not change public track counters until all effects have been added. New losses take effect for future rounds, after this round's assigned actions have been resolved.

1. Confirm assignments and authority

Each faction's two players must confirm matching assignments. If either fails to confirm or they disagree, use Guild defaults Wayfarer Trade / Lantern Maintain, or Janissariat defaults Spear Stand down / Vigil Home. For an agreed sheet with a missing or multiple choice for one asset, use that asset's default. Apply a binding Standing Commitment over any contrary choice or default. Record lost Wayfarer, Lantern or Spear as Absent.

Interim and NI give Navigation authority to the Ascendant; NG gives it to GN; NJ requires GN and JN to agree. Interim and MI give Security authority to the Ascendant; MA gives it to JS; MJ requires GS and JS to agree. Missing, conflicting or unauthorised orders become Hold for Navigation or Restraint for Security, with no penalty for invalidity alone. Collect both final orders before revealing either. The Ascendant gives any Navigation order from Security at the deadline.

Record Grey Petrel's lodged task: Relief lift, Tender cover or Keep. A missing task means Keep. If its reserve was already spent, use Keep. An untriggered task retains the reserve for the next round. Private Trade, Maintain and independent Raid do not require council authorisation.

2. Resolve the Warrant

An effective Warrant requires Interim or MI military authority, opening Readiness at least 3 and Vigil assigned to Council. It uses Vigil first, gives guarded status and adds Stability +1. Mark Vigil used; Security may still use offered Spear for a different order.

Under Interim or MI, a Warrant lacking Readiness or offered Vigil fails: Stability −1. Under MA or MJ it is advice only, with no effect or failure penalty. A missing or unconfirmed Warrant means No. The Warrant itself is not combat.

3. Resolve Guard

If Security ordered Guard and Vigil is offered and unused, mark it used, set guarded status and add Stability +1. Otherwise Guard becomes Restraint and adds Stability −1. This includes a simultaneous Guard when a valid Warrant already used Vigil. Guard does not count as combat.

4. Check Navigation's assets

Relief needs offered Wayfarer. If Wayfarer was not offered and available Grey Petrel has Relief lift lodged, the reserve supplies this voyage and is spent immediately, even if poor Beacon later halts it. Grey Petrel cannot replace a ship for Military or Shared.

Military needs offered Wayfarer. Shared needs both offered Wayfarer and offered Lantern. Repair needs offered Lantern. Hold needs no asset. If an authorised order lacks its requirement, change it to Hold and add Stability −1 once, even if Shared lacks both assets. This failed order cannot also receive the halted-voyage penalty.

5. Prepare the route

Prepared Beacon equals opening Beacon, plus 1 if available Lantern is assigned Maintain, plus 2 for a funded Repair. Cap prepared Beacon at 4. Lantern cannot do both jobs. Keep this prepared value for every voyage in the round; do not subtract crossing wear yet.

6. Resolve the council voyage

For a funded Relief, Military or Shared order, prepared Beacon below 2 halts the voyage. Add Stability −1; deliver no cargo or military passage; add no wear. Otherwise deliver the voyage and add one crossing wear. Relief adds Stability +2. Shared adds Stability +1 and provides military passage. Military provides military passage with no supply bonus. Shared is one voyage, not two.

Repair and Hold make no council crossing. Grey Petrel Relief has the ordinary Relief cargo effect and crossing wear if it departs. It cannot carry Spear for Campaign.

7. Resolve private Trade

If available Wayfarer is assigned Trade, it completes its voyage at prepared Beacon 3–4, or at 2 with guarded status. Add one completed contract and one crossing wear. At prepared Beacon 2 without protection, disable Wayfarer: no contract, but still one wear. Below 2 it stays intact in port: no contract and no wear.

Wayfarer cannot Trade while offered to Council, even if the council leaves it unused. A private Trade and a Grey Petrel Relief voyage may both cross; they use the same prepared Beacon and each adds wear. One commercial opportunity exists per round; a missed contract cannot be made up by doubling Trade later.

8. Apply Charter duties

Each faction takes at most one Stability −1 duty penalty in a round. Duties apply even if the required asset was lost. This is additional to operation failures, fighting, consumption and scenario effects.

Guild under Interim: no service duty. NG: Lantern must Maintain or Navigation must deliver a funded Repair; merely offering Lantern does not suffice. NI or NJ: Wayfarer must be assigned Council, whether it sails or not. Grey Petrel does not satisfy that duty.

Janissariat under Interim: an independent Raid breaks discipline. MI or MJ: Spear must be assigned Council. MA: Vigil must stay Home; an independent Raid is permitted if it does. These duties do not transfer ownership or create an asset that was withheld.

9. Resolve Campaign and Raid

A council Campaign or Raid needs offered Spear. Without it, that council order becomes Restraint and adds Stability −1. A separately assigned independent Raid still resolves and pays its own costs. Never use Spear twice.

Campaign with offered Spear but without delivered Military or Shared passage does not leave: Stability −1, Spear remains intact, no combat. With passage, Campaign still costs Stability −1. At opening Readiness at least 2 it achieves qualifying combat; at 0–1 disable Spear and record no qualifying combat.

For each actual Raid, add Stability −1 for disruption. In round one the target is present only if private Wayfarer Trade completed. In round two it is present only if the authorised funded Navigation order is Relief and the voyage was delivered; Grey Petrel Relief qualifies, but Shared, Military and Trade do not.

If the Raid target is absent, Spear stays intact and there is no combat. If present at opening Readiness at least 2, add a further Stability −1 and record qualifying combat. If present at Readiness 0–1, disable Spear, with no further Stability loss and no qualifying combat. The target's presence never causes a Raid unless one was actually assigned or ordered.

10. Apply the other scenario conditions

Round one: delivered Shared passage without guarded status loses Lantern's navigation team. If available Grey Petrel has Tender cover lodged, prevent that loss and spend the reserve. Guard or a valid Warrant prevents the loss without triggering Tender cover. Tender cover gives no general guarded status and has no round-two trigger.

Round two: delivered Military passage without guarded status adds Stability −1. Tender cover does not protect these stores. Separately, a funded council Repair together with completed private Trade adds Stability +1. Maintain is not Repair. These two round-two effects cannot both trigger because Wayfarer cannot both Trade and serve Military passage.

Apply these conditions whether a vision was bought or not. The private answer cards below restate the conditions. The qualitative handouts reveal the same causal information without giving the arithmetic to players.

11. Finish the tracks and assets

Readiness changes once: +1 if any operation achieved qualifying combat, otherwise −1. Guard, Home, food, an unsuccessful expedition and a Warrant do not count as combat. Final Beacon equals prepared Beacon minus all crossing wear. Final Stability equals opening Stability plus the complete change ledger. Clamp each final track to 0–4 only now.

Mark losses of Wayfarer, Lantern's team and Spear; add completed contracts; mark Grey Petrel spent only when triggered. Vigil stays intact. No available action restores lost assets during this game. Repair affects Beacon only. Surviving assets return to their owners for the next assignment. Expire a Standing Commitment after its round-two operation, not before.

12. Announce the result

Show both final orders, the new track values and their descriptions, lost or surviving assets, contracts and reserve expenditure. Describe events in the fiction: supplies arrived, a convoy was halted, a patrol was intercepted, the cohort failed to find combat, or navigators were lost. Do not display the worksheet, formulas, thresholds, event ledger or unpurchased vision conditions. Players may draw conclusions from experience; do not invent misleading events to conceal a calculation.

Update public settlement weights using the rulebook. The rules for who decides, how to commit an asset, buying foresight and ratifying the settlement remain public. Keep detailed arithmetic in the facilitator record for checking the result and discussing the design after play.

Worked resolution

Begin round one at Beacon 2, Stability 3, Readiness 3. Guild offers Wayfarer and Lantern; the army offers Spear and Vigil. Navigation orders Shared; Security orders Campaign; the Ascendant issues a Warrant and keeps Grey Petrel.

The Warrant uses Vigil, gives guarded status and adds Stability +1. Shared is funded. Lantern is offered, not maintaining, so prepared Beacon stays 2. Shared departs, adds Stability +1 and one wear. There is no Trade and no duty breach. Campaign receives passage and offered Spear, costs Stability −1 and achieves combat. Guarded status prevents the round-one crew loss. Including consumption −1, the Stability change is 0. Finish at Beacon 1, Stability 3 and Readiness 4. All assets survive; no contract is completed; Grey Petrel remains available.

Tell players: civilian supplies and the cohort crossed together; Vigil protected the passage; the Campaign fought successfully; the route is deteriorating after use. Show the final tracks and the missed contract. Keep the separate modifiers on your worksheet.

Fixed visions: facilitator-only answers

The following are the fixed private answers. The public covers name the primary council and other affected councils, without these descriptive titles or specific choices. These effects happen whether or not the Ascendant buys the card. All four answers concern choices still open when foresight is purchased: unlocked asset assignments and Grey Petrel's task can change during subsequent visits, and council orders and the Warrant are decided later. Standing Commitments, losses and lack of authority may limit a particular intervention. Do not promise that a purchase will be useful or refund it if the relevant option is unavailable.

Vision R1-N

The exposed transfer

Shared crosses without protection → If funded Shared passage is delivered and there is no funded Guard or valid Warrant, Lantern’s navigation team is lost. A lodged, available Grey Petrel on Tender cover prevents this loss and is spent.

Shared crosses with protection → A funded Guard or valid Warrant prevents this crew loss. Tender cover does not trigger and stays available.

Shared does not cross → This crew-loss effect does not occur. An unfunded or halted order still has its normal cost.

Shared needs both Wayfarer and Lantern on Council. Passage still needs prepared Beacon 2+. Guard needs offered Vigil; a Warrant also needs imperial authority and opening Readiness 3+. Protection does not repair Beacon or avoid crossing wear.

Vision R1-S

The merchant’s wake

Wayfarer completes private Trade → A Raid finds the target. At opening Readiness 2+, it fights: total Raid cost Stability −2 and the round’s combat change is Readiness +1. At Readiness 0–1, Spear is disabled: Raid costs Stability −1 and there is no qualifying combat from that Raid.

No completed private Trade → The Raid finds no target. It costs Stability −1, Spear remains intact, and it provides no qualifying combat. Council Relief, Military or Shared passage does not substitute for a private contract.

No Raid → These Raid effects do not occur. Campaign and other orders follow their own rules.

A Trade assignment is not enough: the voyage must complete under the public Beacon and protection rules. If no operation achieves combat, Readiness falls by 1 once. Duty breaches and monthly consumption are additional; tracks stay within 0–4.

Vision R2-N

Stores at the crossing

Military passage crosses unguarded → Delivered Military passage without funded Guard or valid Warrant causes an extra Stability −1. Protection prevents this extra loss. Grey Petrel’s Tender cover does not protect these stores.

Funded Repair and completed Trade → A funded council Repair together with a completed private Wayfarer Trade gives Stability +1 from distribution. Both must happen. Lantern must be on Council for Repair; Maintain is not Repair.

Neither condition → Neither extra supply effect applies. Relief and Shared receive their normal cargo effects; other cards and ordinary costs still apply.

Repair adds 2 to prepared Beacon, capped at 4, before voyages. Trade must complete. Military needs Wayfarer on Council, so it cannot also make private Trade. These two extra effects cannot both trigger in the same round.

Vision R2-S

The relief column

Relief is delivered → A Raid finds the target if the authorised, funded Navigation order is Relief and its voyage actually crosses. Grey Petrel Relief lift counts. At opening Readiness 2+, Raid costs Stability −2 total and achieves combat; at 0–1, Spear is disabled, Raid costs Stability −1 and provides no qualifying combat.

Relief is not delivered → The Raid finds no target, costs Stability −1, leaves Spear intact and provides no qualifying combat. A proposed, unfunded or halted Relief order does not qualify. Shared, Military and private Trade do not qualify.

No Raid → Relief alone does not cause an attack or a Raid penalty. These effects require an actual Raid assignment or funded council Raid.

Combat changes Readiness by +1 once; no successful combat changes it by −1 once. Ordinary cargo, Guard, duty breaches and consumption still apply. A council Raid needs offered Spear; an independent Raid uses its own assignment.

Trial and final votes

The round-one trial needs spoken agreement from GN+JN+A for Navigation and GS+JS+A for Military. Read back each exact article and its attached terms, independently of the other domain, and record whether it passes. Up to one asset commitment per faction may be attached, with both owners' spoken agreement. Do not quietly detach an unapproved promise to pass its article. No pledge is compulsory. A passed pledge binds only that asset assignment in round two; an informal public plan has no mechanical penalty.

At the Ceremony freeze actual weights. Count both owners’ votes to produce each faction signature; refusal, missing approval or disagreement contributes no support, but the faction’s weight remains in the total. Threshold is ceil(2 × total / 3); at least two signing parties must have positive weight. A zero electorate cannot pass. Count no contracts, verbal concessions or invented influence.

Show the exact final package with five rows, one for each player, and two boxes per row: Assent and Do not assent. Each player ticks one box. A blank row or both boxes ticked gives no assent. If the package fails, permit a replacement within the deadline and use fresh boxes for all five players. Stop on success. If neither of the two permitted packages passes, both provisional articles expire to Interim; no physical state changes. Record dissent even where the weighted package passes. Passing the arithmetic does not establish political consensus.

Checks and debrief

Cooperation: assets remain scarce, a peaceful month reduces army and Ascendant leverage, and a public ship assignment forgoes a contract. Withheld resources: a directive cannot requisition them automatically. Conflicting orders: check the required decision-makers' agreement before funding. Averted prophecy: a guarded first-round Shared passage or triggered Tender cover prevents the printed crew loss. Failed ratification: expire articles without healing anything.

Ask for starting and ending constitutional preferences and the event that changed them. Ask each player which teammate contribution mattered. For foresight, distinguish assignment changes, changes in the approach to councils, final order changes, bargaining conditions and confidence. A remaining third token is unavoidable if there were two purchases; do not code it as deliberate saving.

The migrated six-agent run found a useful first purchase and a mostly confirmatory second purchase. It did not show deliberate saving or human timing. Do not guide the next Ascendant to repeat that result. Preserve choices before interpreting them.

Prophecy handouts for the Ascendant

Give only the chosen handout after payment. Keep the exact answer and arithmetic above behind your screen. These handouts reveal conditional events, not numerical modifiers.

R1-N — The exposed transfer

If Shared passage is delivered without protection: Lantern's navigation team is lost. Grey Petrel on Tender cover can save the team, using its contracted service.

If Shared passage is delivered with Guard or an effective Warrant: The navigation team survives this threat. Tender cover is not called upon.

If Shared passage is not delivered: This threat does not take the navigation team. A failed or halted operation can still have other consequences.

You can seek protection, change the voyage or arrange Tender cover while the relevant decisions remain open. Protection does not mend the route.

R1-S — The merchant’s wake

If Wayfarer's private Trade voyage completes and Spear makes a Raid: The Raid finds the Sorenne patrol. A force able to sustain combat fights and strengthens its conditioning, at a further cost to civilian life. A force too weakened to fight is disabled instead.

If No private Trade voyage completes and Spear makes a Raid: The patrol is not found. The expedition disrupts civilian traffic without sustaining the cohort through combat. Council cargo does not substitute for a commercial voyage.

If No Raid takes place: These Raid consequences do not occur. Campaign and other orders have their own consequences.

Agreeing to Trade is not enough: the voyage must complete. Consider whether to offer Spear to the council, raid independently or avoid the expedition while those choices remain open.

R2-N — Stores at the crossing

If Military passage is delivered without Guard or an effective Warrant: Stores are lost in the loading arrangements, worsening civilian shortages. Protection prevents this extra loss; Tender cover cannot protect the stores.

If Council Repair is carried out and private Wayfarer Trade completes: The repaired route and completed commercial voyage improve distribution to Sorenne’s Fall. Both must happen. Routine Maintain work is not this Repair operation.

If Neither condition occurs: Neither of these additional supply effects occurs. Other cargo, operation and scenario effects still apply.

You can seek a different use of the ships or protection while the relevant assignments and orders remain open. This vision promises no extra benefit for an ordinary Relief voyage.

R2-S — The relief column

If Relief is delivered and Spear makes a Raid: The relief traffic brings the Sorenne patrol within reach. A force able to sustain combat fights, strengthening its conditioning but further disrupting civilian life. A force too weakened to fight is disabled instead. Grey Petrel Relief also draws the patrol.

If Relief is not delivered and Spear makes a Raid: The patrol is not found. The expedition disrupts civilian traffic without achieving combat. Shared passage, Military passage and private Trade do not draw this patrol.

If No Raid takes place: Relief alone does not cause an attack. The army must actually launch a Raid for these Raid consequences to occur.

Consider the Navigation order and whether Spear will raid. Council orders remain open after assignments lock, but an independent Raid cannot then be cancelled by a council.
