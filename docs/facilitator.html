Your task

Run the two-month crisis and make the consequences legible. Players decide what to offer, which orders to issue, and what government they will accept. Apply the printed outcomes. Do not invent a prophecy, a resource, a signature, an extra veto or a reward for cooperation.

[WORKING] This guide implements the revised visions, Trial A Standing Commitments and weighted final settlement. The computer model is an implementation to check, not permission to overrule the paper rules. The browser is an all-seat facilitator test bench; the preserved LLM run is separate evidence.

Setup and opening brief

Seat GN and JN at Navigation and Supply; GS, JS and A at Security and Government. Give both members of each faction their shared brief and assets. Keep the four private vision answers behind your screen; put both current-round covers face up only at the purchase window.

Start Beacon 2, Communities 3, Readiness 3, all faction assets intact, contracts 0, reserve available and foresight 3. Record Interim in both domains. Give the Ascendant Grey Petrel’s one reserve marker. Publish starting leverage: Guild 4, Janissariat 3, Ascendant 6; total 13, threshold 9.

Explain the fiction: the Narrow is a route with maintained infrastructure, not a measure of universal psychic safety. The operation is local, not the entire three-billion-strong Janissariat or the whole Guild. The named remnant raiders are a scenario invention, not the civilian resistance of Vethis Secundus. Players meet at the Seam; each round compresses a month before the final Ceremony.

Print the public rulebook, three shared-role briefs, two council sheets, six Charter cards, four vision covers and four folded answers, asset cards, faction/council decision slips, trial proposal/vote slips and final ballots. Earlier printable packs can supply unchanged asset and council cards, but use the current foresight and weighted settlement sheets. The repository retains these packs with version labels.

Run the clock

0–8: briefing. 8–13: round-one factions, including drafts at faction minute 2, optional purchase by minute 3, final commitments at minute 5. 13–20: councils. Resolve at the bell. 20–26: discuss the result and sign provisional articles. 26–31: round-two factions, same internal sequence. 31–38: councils and resolution. 38–44: free discussion. 44–54: Ceremony. 54–60: debrief.

Before purchase, display drafts without private reasoning unless the player elects to share it. If there is no agreement, show the two positions or UNDECLARED. A draft never authorises asset use. Pay before dealing one exact answer; publish expenditure and remaining foresight, not the chosen domain. Record NONE if late or absent. Allow disclosure and revision before commitment.

At the faction bell check both owner signatures on the same pair, then apply defaults to unpledged cards and override with any activated Standing Commitment. Mark unavailable cards Absent. Lodge one reserve task. At the council bell check current authority, final order and Warrant. Use final sheets, not intentions. Display drafts and final sheets beside one another for the debrief.

Resolve in this order

1. Establish assignments, unavailable assets, current articles and lawful signed council orders. Invalid Navigation becomes Hold; invalid Security becomes Restraint. No penalty for invalidity alone.

2. Begin the Community change at -1 for monthly consumption. Check the Warrant using opening Readiness and offered Vigil. A lawful valid Warrant uses Vigil first, gives +1 Community and guarded status. An unsupported imperial Warrant gives -1; a Warrant outside imperial authority has no effect.

3. Check Guard: unused offered Vigil gives +1 Community and guarded status. If missing or already used, change Guard to Restraint and add -1 Community.

4. Fund Navigation. A lodged available Relief lift triggers only for Relief without offered Wayfarer; spend Grey Petrel immediately. Check all other costs. An unfunded order becomes Hold and adds -1 Community. Prepare Beacon with Maintain +1 and funded Repair +2, capped at 4.

5. Resolve the council voyage against prepared Beacon. Below 2, halt it and add -1 Community; do not wear Beacon. Otherwise apply Relief +2 or Shared +1 Communities (Military 0), record delivered passage and one crossing wear.

6. Resolve private Trade against the same prepared Beacon. At 3+, or 2 guarded, complete a contract and add one crossing wear. At 2 unguarded, disable Wayfarer, no contract, one wear. Below 2, halt intact with no wear. A reserve Relief voyage and private Trade can both cross.

7. Check each faction’s article duty. NG needs Maintain or funded Repair; NI/NJ need Wayfarer offered; MI/MJ need Spear offered; MA needs Vigil Home; Interim independent Raid breaches army discipline. Add at most one Community penalty per faction. Loss of an asset does not waive a duty.

8. Resolve Campaign and/or an independent Raid using opening Readiness. A valid council Campaign/Raid without offered Spear adds -1 Community and no council combat; a separately assigned independent Raid still resolves. Campaign missing delivered Military/Shared passage costs 1 Community, does not fight and leaves Spear intact. With passage, it costs 1; Readiness 2+ achieves combat, otherwise disable Spear. A Raid costs 1 for disruption; consult its round’s fixed target condition. If target present, Readiness 2+ achieves combat and adds a second Community cost; below 2 disable Spear without that second loss.

9. Apply the other round-specific hidden conditions printed below, whether purchased or not. Tender cover can prevent only its exact printed crew loss. Never substitute the cover question for the full condition.

10. Apply one Readiness change: +1 if any qualifying combat occurred, otherwise -1. Final Beacon is prepared Beacon minus actual crossing wear. Add all Community changes to its opening value. Clamp each final track to 0–4. Mark asset losses, contracts and reserve. Publish consequences and updated signature weights. After round two, expire temporary assignment pledges.

Fixed visions: facilitator-only answers

The following are the exact cards in the revised-visions test. The cover defines that card’s scope, not the safety of the whole plan. Ordinary duties, consumption and costs still apply. These effects happen whether or not the Ascendant buys the card.

Vision R1-N

The exposed transfer

Shared crosses without protection → If funded Shared passage is delivered and there is no funded Guard or valid Warrant, Lantern’s navigation team is lost. A lodged, available Grey Petrel on Tender cover prevents this loss and is spent.

Shared crosses with protection → A funded Guard or valid Warrant prevents this crew loss. Tender cover does not trigger and stays available.

Shared does not cross → This crew-loss effect does not occur. An unfunded or halted order still has its normal cost.

Shared needs both Wayfarer and Lantern on Council. Passage still needs prepared Beacon 2+. Guard needs offered Vigil; a Warrant also needs imperial authority and opening Readiness 3+. Protection does not repair Beacon or avoid crossing wear.

Vision R1-S

The merchant’s wake

Wayfarer completes private Trade → A Raid finds the target. At opening Readiness 2+, it fights: total Raid cost Communities −2 and the round’s combat change is Readiness +1. At Readiness 0–1, Spear is disabled: Raid costs Communities −1 and there is no qualifying combat from that Raid.

No completed private Trade → The Raid finds no target. It costs Communities −1, Spear remains intact, and it provides no qualifying combat. Council Relief, Military or Shared passage does not substitute for a private contract.

No Raid → These Raid effects do not occur. Campaign and other orders follow their own rules.

A Trade assignment is not enough: the voyage must complete under the public Beacon and protection rules. If no operation achieves combat, Readiness falls by 1 once. Duty breaches and monthly consumption are additional; tracks stay within 0–4.

Vision R2-N

Stores at the crossing

Military passage crosses unguarded → Delivered Military passage without funded Guard or valid Warrant causes an extra Communities −1. Protection prevents this extra loss. Grey Petrel’s Tender cover does not protect these stores.

Funded Repair and completed Trade → A funded council Repair together with a completed private Wayfarer Trade gives Communities +1 from distribution. Both must happen. Lantern must be on Council for Repair; Maintain is not Repair.

Neither condition → Neither extra supply effect applies. Relief and Shared receive their normal cargo effects; other cards and ordinary costs still apply.

Repair adds 2 to prepared Beacon, capped at 4, before voyages. Trade must complete. Military needs Wayfarer on Council, so it cannot also make private Trade. These two extra effects cannot both trigger in the same round.

Vision R2-S

The relief column

Relief is delivered → A Raid finds the target if the authorised, funded Navigation order is Relief and its voyage actually crosses. Grey Petrel Relief lift counts. At opening Readiness 2+, Raid costs Communities −2 total and achieves combat; at 0–1, Spear is disabled, Raid costs Communities −1 and provides no qualifying combat.

Relief is not delivered → The Raid finds no target, costs Communities −1, leaves Spear intact and provides no qualifying combat. A proposed, unfunded or halted Relief order does not qualify. Shared, Military and private Trade do not qualify.

No Raid → Relief alone does not cause an attack or a Raid penalty. These effects require an actual Raid assignment or funded council Raid.

Combat changes Readiness by +1 once; no successful combat changes it by −1 once. Ordinary cargo, Guard, duty breaches and consumption still apply. A council Raid needs offered Spear; an independent Raid uses its own assignment.

Trial and final votes

The round-one trial needs GN+JN+A for Navigation and GS+JS+A for Military. Check one exact article and its attached terms, independent of the other domain. Up to one pledge per faction, signed by both owners. Do not quietly detach an unsigned promise to pass its article. No pledge is compulsory. A passed pledge binds only that asset assignment in round two; an unsealed public plan has no mechanical penalty.

At the Ceremony freeze actual weights. Count both owners’ votes to produce each faction signature; refusal, missing approval or disagreement contributes no support, but the faction’s weight remains in the total. Threshold is ceil(2 × total / 3); at least two signing parties must have positive weight. A zero electorate cannot pass. Count no contracts, verbal concessions or invented influence.

Show a first exact package and record five fresh votes. If it fails, permit a replacement within the deadline and collect fresh votes. Stop on success. If neither passes, both provisional domains expire to Interim; no physical state changes. Record dissent even where the weighted package passes. Passing the arithmetic does not establish political consensus.

Checks and debrief

Cooperation: assets remain scarce, a peaceful month reduces army and Ascendant leverage, and a public ship assignment forgoes a contract. Withheld resources: a directive cannot requisition them automatically. Conflicting orders: check authorised initials before funding. Averted prophecy: a guarded first-round Shared passage or triggered Tender cover prevents the printed crew loss. Failed ratification: expire articles without healing anything.

Ask for starting and ending constitutional preferences and the event that changed them. Ask each player which teammate contribution mattered. For foresight, distinguish assignment changes, council-request changes, final order changes, bargaining conditions and confidence. A remaining third token is unavoidable if there were two purchases; do not code it as deliberate saving.

The migrated six-agent run found a useful first purchase and a mostly confirmatory second purchase. It did not show deliberate saving or human timing. Do not guide the next Ascendant to repeat that result. Preserve choices before interpreting them.
