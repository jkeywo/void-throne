The Void Throne — Player Briefs

Give the Guild and Janissariat players their faction’s shared brief. Give the Ascendant their own brief. Read these alongside the player rulebook.

The Ductus Guild

Two players · shared assets and objectives. Your Navigation delegate is GN; your Security delegate is GS.

Use the faction phase to agree asset assignments and negotiating priorities with your teammate. Stay at your own table; do not speak or send messages to the other faction. The Ascendant may visit after making their foresight choice. You may discuss proposals with them and revise unlocked assignments until the phase ends. Both players then confirm the same assignments aloud and record them without signatures. Take your approach to the councils yourselves, without written instructions. During councils, remain at your assigned table and speak only with that council. You cannot consult your teammate at the other council or send messages between them. Agree any plan that depends on both councils beforehand; each council still makes its own final decision.

You operate the passage service through the Narrow. Wayfarer is your freight vessel; Lantern carries your navigation team and maintains the route. Those ships are needed for civilian supplies, military transport and your own commercial obligations.

Your petition, GUILD-14, asks the Charter to recognise the Guild's navigation rights. Each Navigation article answers it differently: Guild discretion leaves priorities with you, Imperial priority places them with the Ascendant, and Joint authorisation gives both factions a say.

Your objectives are to:

Keep Lantern's navigators alive and finish with Beacon at least 2.

Secure Guild navigation rights, preferably through Guild discretion.

Complete two commercial contracts, one in each round.

Leave Sorenne's Fall with Stability at least 2.

Offering Wayfarer for public service means missing that month's contract. The ship returns if it survives, but the opportunity has gone. You may accept that cost for relief, protection or a worthwhile constitutional concession.

The Janissariat

Two players · shared assets and objectives. Your Navigation delegate is JN; your Security delegate is JS.

Use the faction phase to agree asset assignments and negotiating priorities with your teammate. Stay at your own table; do not speak or send messages to the other faction. The Ascendant may visit after making their foresight choice. You may discuss proposals with them and revise unlocked assignments until the phase ends. Both players then confirm the same assignments aloud and record them without signatures. Take your approach to the councils yourselves, without written instructions. During councils, remain at your assigned table and speak only with that council. You cannot consult your teammate at the other council or send messages between them. Agree any plan that depends on both councils beforehand; each council still makes its own final decision.

You command Spear, the 9th Pale Cohort, and Vigil, the Narrow garrison. Spear has local assault craft, but needs Guild transport to campaign beyond the crossing. Vigil can protect traffic or remain at its institutional bases.

The Sorenne Flag gives you an enemy to fight. The difficulty is finding it without further damaging the system you are supposed to secure. An unsuccessful expedition still consumes supplies and disrupts civilian traffic, while doing nothing to sustain your conditioning.

Your objectives are to:

Keep Spear intact and finish with Readiness at least 3, preferably 4.

Secure the army's constitutional position, preferably through Military autonomy.

Protect your soldiers from operations they cannot sustain. A weakened cohort risks being destroyed by the fighting meant to sustain it.

Leave Sorenne's Fall with Stability at least 2.

Military autonomy lets you choose operations, but requires Vigil to stay Home. Joint authorisation gives the Guild a say over military orders. Imperial command leaves the Ascendant able to order you to stand down.

The Ascendant

One player · A on decision sheets. Attend Security and Government and remain there throughout the council phase. When you hold Navigation authority, give its final order to the facilitator at the deadline from your own table. The facilitator records it; you do not visit Navigation or send it messages. No signature is needed.

You need a government capable of maintaining the Dominion, and an army capable of defending it. A quiet month may help Sorenne's Fall while weakening both the Janissariat and the authority they give you.

You begin with imperial command, three foresight tokens and one contracted service from the cutter Grey Petrel. Spending foresight gives you information about the crisis, but reduces your weight in the final settlement.

Your objectives are to:

Ratify a settlement with Beacon at least 1, Stability at least 2 and Readiness at least 2.

Keep Readiness at least 3 and Spear intact where the cost is acceptable.

Establish a government you can trust to govern without your constant intervention.

Account for the people and institutions that paid for that settlement.

You gain no special success from keeping imperial command. Use the provisional government to find out whether delegation works, then judge the final Charter on that evidence.

Your powers

Use your worksheet to record your foresight choice and Grey Petrel's task during faction discussion, and your final Warrant choice during councils. Confirm your final decisions to the facilitator from Security and Government; you do not need to write instructions or sign an order. Your authority over council decisions follows the current Charter articles, described in the Component Reference.

Foresight

The Ascendant starts with three foresight tokens. Each unspent token adds one to their final settlement weight.

At the start of each faction phase, choose your foresight before visiting either faction or hearing its plans for this round. Read the two covers: Navigation and Supply, and Security and Government. They identify the council at the centre of each prophecy and any other council it could affect. They do not reveal the action, condition, risk or reward. Choose one answer and pay one token before opening it, or choose NONE and retain your token. You may use the public state, existing commitments and anything learned in earlier phases to make this choice.

There is at most one purchase per round. Your choice must reach the facilitator before you visit a faction and before the faction phase ends. A missing, multiple or late choice means NONE. Choosing NONE also completes your choice and permits visits. Tokens do not recover, and there are no swaps, refunds or further purchases that month. Waiting to choose leaves less time for visits; it does not extend the phase.

The facilitator announces the expenditure and remaining tokens. The chosen card and its answer remain private unless the Ascendant shares them. You may show, describe, conceal or misrepresent the answer; the facilitator will not certify your claims to other players.

An answer describes reliable events if its conditions occur, without giving the facilitator’s numerical calculations. It does not tell you what players will choose or cover every possible risk. Those conditions apply whether anyone reads the card or not.

While you choose, the two factions discuss privately with their own teammates. After your choice is recorded, you may visit either faction, return to one you visited earlier, relay offers and share or withhold your vision. Each visit is a conversation with that faction; do not bring the two factions together. Until the phase ends, factions can revise unlocked assignments and you can revise Grey Petrel's task. Existing Standing Commitments remain binding. Council orders and your Warrant are decided in the council phase. Avoiding a vision's condition averts its result; it does not make the vision false.

Example: you choose the Navigation prophecy before hearing the Guild's plan. During a later visit, you discover they intend a voyage mentioned in the answer. You can explain its consequences and ask for a different assignment or protection. The cover did not promise that their plan would make this prophecy useful. A lost asset or a Standing Commitment may limit what can change; spending a token does not guarantee a useful intervention.

There are three tokens and at most one purchase in each of the two rounds. At least one token will remain. Keeping more requires choosing NONE in at least one round.

Imperial Warrant

The Warrant draws on the Ascendant's authority over the army to use Vigil independently of the council's other operation.

Requirement: Military authority is Interim or Imperial command and Vigil is offered to Council. A weakened army may be unable to carry out the Warrant; the facilitator resolves this from the current situation.

Timing: record the final Warrant before the council bell.

Purpose: use Vigil to protect traffic and service installations under imperial authority. An effective Warrant takes precedence over the council’s use of Vigil. Security may still use offered Spear for another operation.

Under Interim or Imperial command, an unsupported Warrant can fail and disrupt the system. Under Military autonomy or Joint authorisation it is advice only. A missing or unconfirmed Warrant means No.

Vigil cannot serve both a Warrant and Guard. Plan which authority will use the garrison.

Grey Petrel

Grey Petrel is a contracted cutter with a licensed Guild pilot and a protection detail. Its contract covers one triggered intervention during the whole game. It cannot carry Spear, repair Beacon or provide a second service after its token is spent.

Before the faction bell, lodge Relief lift, Tender cover or Keep. The task locks before councils. If its condition does not occur, retain the reserve token.

Relief lift holds Grey Petrel ready to supply the Navigation council’s Relief voyage if Wayfarer is not offered. It cannot provide military transport. Calling on its contracted service uses the reserve even if the voyage later cannot depart; Wayfarer may still pursue Trade independently.

Tender cover holds Grey Petrel ready to protect Lantern’s navigation team during the first month’s Shared passage. It is a specific rescue service, not general convoy protection. If called upon, it uses the reserve. The service has no second-month use.

Keep retains the token without intervening. A missing task also means Keep. Unspent reserve gives no settlement weight.
