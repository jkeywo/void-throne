The Void Throne — Facilitator Guide, draft v0.3

Your task

Run the two-month crisis and make the consequences legible. Players decide what to offer, which orders to issue, and what government they will accept. Apply the printed outcomes. Do not invent a prophecy, a resource, another player's agreement, an extra veto or a reward for cooperation.

[WORKING] This guide implements the revised visions, Trial A Standing Commitments and weighted final settlement. The computer model is an implementation to check, not permission to overrule the paper rules. The browser is an all-seat facilitator test bench; the preserved LLM run is separate evidence.

Setup and opening brief

Materials

One player rulebook, one Component Reference, one facilitator guide and three shared-role briefs. The Ascendant's brief includes their complete foresight, Warrant and reserve rules. Give each council the relevant action and Charter references from the Component Reference.

Two council reference sheets and four asset cards: Wayfarer, Lantern, Spear and Vigil.

Six Charter cards: three Navigation articles and three Military articles.

Four vision covers and four concealed answers: Navigation and Security for each of the two rounds.

Three tracks numbered 0–4, with one counter each; three foresight tokens; one reserve token for Grey Petrel.

Four faction sheets and four council sheets: one for each faction and council in each round.

Two Ascendant worksheets, one provisional-government proposal and outcome record, two final-package ballot sheets, and a public record for results and contracts.

Blank paper can replace a decision sheet if it records the same choices and outcomes. Use the current vision and settlement rules with any older printed components.

Arrange the table

Place Navigation and Supply at one end and Security and Government at the other. Keep the tracks, current articles and recorded decisions in a central space where everyone can read them. Factions need room to confer between council meetings.

Seat GN and JN at Navigation and Supply; GS, JS and A at Security and Government. Give both members of each faction their shared brief. Keep the four private vision answers behind your screen; put both current-round covers face up at the start of faction discussion.

Starting position

Give Wayfarer and Lantern to the Guild, and Spear and Vigil to the Janissariat. All four assets begin available. Give the Ascendant three foresight tokens and Grey Petrel's reserve token. The facilitator keeps the vision answers concealed.

Set Beacon to 2, Sorenne's Fall's Stability to 3 and Readiness to 3. The Guild has completed no contracts. Write Interim in both article spaces: the Ascendant chooses both council orders, while the factions control their own assets.

The starting settlement weights are Guild 4, Janissariat 3 and Ascendant 6. If those values remained unchanged, nine of the total thirteen weight would be required to pass a settlement.

Prepare the decision sheets

The faction sheet records the round and one agreed assignment for each asset. Both teammates confirm their choices aloud; no signatures are needed. Representatives take their faction's approach to the councils themselves, without written instructions or requests.

The council sheet records the round, current article and final order. Check spoken agreement from whoever holds authority under that article; do not collect initials. The Ascendant confirms their decisions in person. Record their foresight choice, reserve task and final Warrant on their worksheet.

The public record shows the three tracks, available assets, completed contracts, remaining tokens, current articles and any binding commitments. Keep the draft and final sheets beside it so everyone can see what changed.

Opening brief

Allow eight minutes for the briefing. Explain the factions, the two councils and the crisis before taking the players through a round.

The crisis concerns Sorenne's Fall, the southern star system of forty million people described in the lore, and the Sorenne Flag, House Sorenne's surviving fleet. Stability measures the system's supplies and functioning services, not its loyalty. The Flag also makes irregular supply deliveries there; military disruption can therefore harm the people the Dominion is trying to govern. For this scenario, their supply route uses the Narrow, Spear seeks passage to a Flag anchorage beyond it, and a Flag patrol supplies the local Raid target. Those deployments are scenario additions. Players remain at the Seam; each round represents one month.

Run the clock

0–8: briefing. 8–13: round-one faction discussion. 13–18: councils and resolution. 18–23: discuss the result and agree provisional articles. 23–28: round-two faction discussion. 28–33: councils and resolution. 33–38: free discussion. 38–48: Ceremony. Use the remaining twelve minutes for the debrief. These five-minute discussion phases follow the current player rulebook.

The faction phase has one five-minute deadline. Players may discuss their approach with teammates, the Ascendant and the other faction from the start, and budget that time themselves. Do not call minute-two or minute-three gates. Before the foresight choice, ask players to explain intended assignments and possible council decisions aloud without private reasoning unless the player elects to share it. If there is no agreement, hear both positions or record no declared intention. An intention never authorises asset use.

The Ascendant chooses when to request one answer after intentions are visible and before the faction bell, or may choose NONE. Pay before dealing the exact answer; publish expenditure and remaining foresight, not the chosen domain. A choice completes that round's opportunity: no swaps or second purchase. Record NONE if no single valid choice arrives before the bell. Discussion continues during the choice and after disclosure; remind players to leave time for final decisions.

At the faction bell, ask both faction members to confirm the same assignments. Record one final faction sheet. If either has not confirmed or they disagree, apply the faction defaults; an activated Standing Commitment still overrides them. Mark unavailable assets Absent and record one reserve task. At the council bell, check the current authority and ask the required decision-makers to confirm the final order; also confirm the Warrant. Resolve the recorded decisions. No signatures or initials are needed.

In the browser, sharing intentions, recording the foresight choice and collecting final decisions are successive recording steps within the same faction phase. Advance the first two when players are ready, without restarting the clock or stopping discussion. Only closing final commitments ends faction discussion.

Resolve in this order

1. Establish assignments, unavailable assets, current articles and lawfully agreed council orders. Invalid Navigation becomes Hold; invalid Security becomes Restraint. No penalty for invalidity alone.

2. Begin the Stability change at -1 for monthly consumption. Check the Warrant using opening Readiness and offered Vigil. A lawful valid Warrant uses Vigil first, gives +1 Stability and guarded status. An unsupported imperial Warrant gives -1; a Warrant outside imperial authority has no effect.

3. Check Guard: unused offered Vigil gives +1 Stability and guarded status. If missing or already used, change Guard to Restraint and add -1 Stability.

4. Fund Navigation. A lodged available Relief lift triggers only for Relief without offered Wayfarer; spend Grey Petrel immediately. Check all other costs. An unfunded order becomes Hold and adds -1 Stability. Prepare Beacon with Maintain +1 and funded Repair +2, capped at 4.

5. Resolve the council voyage against prepared Beacon. Below 2, halt it and add -1 Stability; do not wear Beacon. Otherwise apply Relief +2 or Shared +1 Stability (Military 0), record delivered passage and one crossing wear.

6. Resolve private Trade against the same prepared Beacon. At 3+, or 2 guarded, complete a contract and add one crossing wear. At 2 unguarded, disable Wayfarer, no contract, one wear. Below 2, halt intact with no wear. A reserve Relief voyage and private Trade can both cross.

7. Check each faction’s article duty. NG needs Maintain or funded Repair; NI/NJ need Wayfarer offered; MI/MJ need Spear offered; MA needs Vigil Home; Interim independent Raid breaches army discipline. Add at most one Stability penalty per faction. Loss of an asset does not waive a duty.

8. Resolve Campaign and/or an independent Raid using opening Readiness. A valid council Campaign/Raid without offered Spear adds -1 Stability and no council combat; a separately assigned independent Raid still resolves. Campaign missing delivered Military/Shared passage costs 1 Stability, does not fight and leaves Spear intact. With passage, it costs 1; Readiness 2+ achieves combat, otherwise disable Spear. A Raid costs 1 for disruption; consult its round’s fixed target condition. If target present, Readiness 2+ achieves combat and adds a second Stability cost; below 2 disable Spear without that second loss.

9. Apply the other round-specific hidden conditions printed below, whether purchased or not. Tender cover can prevent only its exact printed crew loss. Never substitute the cover question for the full condition.

10. Apply one Readiness change: +1 if any qualifying combat occurred, otherwise -1. Final Beacon is prepared Beacon minus actual crossing wear. Add all Stability changes to its opening value. Clamp each final track to 0–4. Mark asset losses, contracts and reserve. Publish consequences and updated signature weights. After round two, expire temporary assignment pledges.

Fixed visions: facilitator-only answers

The following are the exact cards in the revised-visions test. The cover defines that card’s scope, not the safety of the whole plan. Ordinary duties, consumption and costs still apply. These effects happen whether or not the Ascendant buys the card.

Vision R1-N

The exposed transfer

Shared crosses without protection → If funded Shared passage is delivered and there is no funded Guard or valid Warrant, Lantern’s navigation team is lost. A lodged, available Grey Petrel on Tender cover prevents this loss and is spent.

Shared crosses with protection → A funded Guard or valid Warrant prevents this crew loss. Tender cover does not trigger and stays available.

Shared does not cross → This crew-loss effect does not occur. An unfunded or halted order still has its normal cost.

Shared needs both Wayfarer and Lantern on Council. Passage still needs prepared Beacon 2+. Guard needs offered Vigil; a Warrant also needs imperial authority and opening Readiness 3+. Protection does not repair Beacon or avoid crossing wear.

Vision R1-S

The merchant’s wake

Wayfarer completes private Trade → A Raid finds the target. At opening Readiness 2+, it fights: total Raid cost Stability −2 and the round’s combat change is Readiness +1. At Readiness 0–1, Spear is disabled: Raid costs Stability −1 and there is no qualifying combat from that Raid.

No completed private Trade → The Raid finds no target. It costs Stability −1, Spear remains intact, and it provides no qualifying combat. Council Relief, Military or Shared passage does not substitute for a private contract.

No Raid → These Raid effects do not occur. Campaign and other orders follow their own rules.

A Trade assignment is not enough: the voyage must complete under the public Beacon and protection rules. If no operation achieves combat, Readiness falls by 1 once. Duty breaches and monthly consumption are additional; tracks stay within 0–4.

Vision R2-N

Stores at the crossing

Military passage crosses unguarded → Delivered Military passage without funded Guard or valid Warrant causes an extra Stability −1. Protection prevents this extra loss. Grey Petrel’s Tender cover does not protect these stores.

Funded Repair and completed Trade → A funded council Repair together with a completed private Wayfarer Trade gives Stability +1 from distribution. Both must happen. Lantern must be on Council for Repair; Maintain is not Repair.

Neither condition → Neither extra supply effect applies. Relief and Shared receive their normal cargo effects; other cards and ordinary costs still apply.

Repair adds 2 to prepared Beacon, capped at 4, before voyages. Trade must complete. Military needs Wayfarer on Council, so it cannot also make private Trade. These two extra effects cannot both trigger in the same round.

Vision R2-S

The relief column

Relief is delivered → A Raid finds the target if the authorised, funded Navigation order is Relief and its voyage actually crosses. Grey Petrel Relief lift counts. At opening Readiness 2+, Raid costs Stability −2 total and achieves combat; at 0–1, Spear is disabled, Raid costs Stability −1 and provides no qualifying combat.

Relief is not delivered → The Raid finds no target, costs Stability −1, leaves Spear intact and provides no qualifying combat. A proposed, unfunded or halted Relief order does not qualify. Shared, Military and private Trade do not qualify.

No Raid → Relief alone does not cause an attack or a Raid penalty. These effects require an actual Raid assignment or funded council Raid.

Combat changes Readiness by +1 once; no successful combat changes it by −1 once. Ordinary cargo, Guard, duty breaches and consumption still apply. A council Raid needs offered Spear; an independent Raid uses its own assignment.

Trial and final votes

The round-one trial needs spoken agreement from GN+JN+A for Navigation and GS+JS+A for Military. Read back each exact article and its attached terms, independently of the other domain, and record whether it passes. Up to one asset commitment per faction may be attached, with both owners' spoken agreement. Do not quietly detach an unapproved promise to pass its article. No pledge is compulsory. A passed pledge binds only that asset assignment in round two; an informal public plan has no mechanical penalty.

At the Ceremony freeze actual weights. Count both owners’ votes to produce each faction signature; refusal, missing approval or disagreement contributes no support, but the faction’s weight remains in the total. Threshold is ceil(2 × total / 3); at least two signing parties must have positive weight. A zero electorate cannot pass. Count no contracts, verbal concessions or invented influence.

Show the exact final package with five rows, one for each player, and two boxes per row: Assent and Do not assent. Each player ticks one box. A blank row or both boxes ticked gives no assent. If the package fails, permit a replacement within the deadline and use fresh boxes for all five players. Stop on success. If neither of the two permitted packages passes, both provisional articles expire to Interim; no physical state changes. Record dissent even where the weighted package passes. Passing the arithmetic does not establish political consensus.

Checks and debrief

Cooperation: assets remain scarce, a peaceful month reduces army and Ascendant leverage, and a public ship assignment forgoes a contract. Withheld resources: a directive cannot requisition them automatically. Conflicting orders: check the required decision-makers' agreement before funding. Averted prophecy: a guarded first-round Shared passage or triggered Tender cover prevents the printed crew loss. Failed ratification: expire articles without healing anything.

Ask for starting and ending constitutional preferences and the event that changed them. Ask each player which teammate contribution mattered. For foresight, distinguish assignment changes, changes in the approach to councils, final order changes, bargaining conditions and confidence. A remaining third token is unavoidable if there were two purchases; do not code it as deliberate saving.

The migrated six-agent run found a useful first purchase and a mostly confirmatory second purchase. It did not show deliberate saving or human timing. Do not guide the next Ascendant to repeat that result. Preserve choices before interpreting them.
