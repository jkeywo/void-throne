The Void Throne — Player Rulebook, draft v0.3

A government worth leaving behind

The conquest is complete. The peace has not begun. At the Seam, the Ascendant, the Ductus Guild and the Janissariat must settle who will govern the routes and who will command the army. Meanwhile, the Narrow is failing, troops need passage, and the same ships cannot carry every cargo.

[WORKING] This is the five-player paper prototype: two Guild players, two Janissariat players and one Ascendant, with a separate facilitator. Allow 60 minutes. Two operational rounds represent successive months before the Ceremony. You need cards, slips, three track counters, three foresight tokens and one reserve token. No computer is needed.

Contents: your roles; the assets and tracks; the sequence of play; orders; government; settlement; reference and debrief.

Your roles

Ductus Guild: protect navigators, obtain a response to Navigation Rights Petition GUILD-14, preserve navigation rights, and keep your commercial institution viable. Your faction owns Wayfarer and Lantern. You decide your priorities together; you do not have individual secret goals.

Janissariat: protect Spear, sustain the conditioning that makes your army effective, and secure a constitutional future for the institution. You own Spear and Vigil. Peaceful service can protect people while reducing your military power. An independent Raid is possible without another faction’s permission; its risks and legal costs still apply.

Ascendant: build a government that can function without repeated personal intervention. Seek a ratified settlement with Beacon at least 1, Communities at least 2 and Readiness at least 2; prefer Readiness 3+ and intact Spear where the cost is acceptable. Retaining imperial command is not a victory requirement. Your three unspent foresight tokens contribute to your final leverage.

Each faction sends one member to Navigation and Supply (GN and JN) and one to Security and Government (GS and JS). The Ascendant attends Security and sends written Navigation directives. Council delegates may use only assets actually offered by their owners. A legal duty does not physically confiscate a ship or prevent disobedience.

The Narrow: tracks and concrete assets

Start Beacon 2, Communities 3 and Readiness 3. All tracks run from 0 to 4. Beacon measures this route’s maintained service and access infrastructure, not the nature of the immaterial deep. Communities measure the welfare of people around the corridor. Readiness measures this force’s conditioned military effectiveness; a lower value is not a judgment that its people are worth less.

Wayfarer: Guild transport. Assign Council to offer it for an imperial voyage, or Trade for a private commercial voyage. It can do only one of these each round. Trade completes one contract at prepared Beacon 3+, or at 2 with Guard/Warrant. At 2 without protection it is disabled and earns no contract; below 2 it stays intact but does not depart. Contracts cannot be spent as resources.

Lantern: Guild tender and navigation team. Assign Maintain for Beacon +1 before crossings, or Council to enable Repair or Shared passage. Maintain does not also fund a council order. If its crew are lost, Lantern is unavailable for the rest of the prototype.

Spear: Janissariat cohort. Assign Council, independent Raid, or Stand down. Council offers it for Campaign or a council Raid; an independent Raid is unavailable to the council. Stand down grants no recovery. A disabled Spear cannot act again.

Vigil: Janissariat garrison. Assign Council for Guard or an eligible Warrant, or Home to protect institutional bases. Home grants no numerical bonus. Vigil remains intact in this prototype and can have only one use per round.

Grey Petrel: one already-contracted cutter with a licensed Guild pilot and protection detail. The Ascendant has one reserve service for the entire game. It does not create navigation expertise. A surviving faction asset returns after service; offering Wayfarer loses that round’s trading opportunity, not the ship or its ownership leverage.

The 60-minute session

Briefing: 8 minutes. Each round: faction discussion 5 minutes, councils 7 minutes, free discussion 6 minutes. Play two rounds, then a 10-minute Ceremony and a 6-minute debrief.

Faction minutes 0–2: discuss your assets and desired orders. At minute 2 display each faction’s draft assignments and requests, plus the Ascendant’s draft directives and Warrant. If teammates disagree, display both proposals. Drafts do not bind. Mark an existing Standing Commitment LOCKED.

Faction minutes 2–3: the Ascendant reads the two public vision covers and chooses one card or NONE. Cost: one foresight token, paid before opening the card. At most one purchase per round. Missing, multiple or late choice means NONE. No later replacement, refund or second purchase.

Faction minutes 3–5: the Ascendant may show, describe or withhold the purchased answer. Revise unpledged assignments, then both teammates sign their exact faction sheet. Lodge Grey Petrel’s task by minute 5. The chosen card predicts consequences only if its printed conditions occur; it never predicts a player’s choice or grants authority. Hidden consequences occur whether read or unread.

Faction defaults: unsigned or disagreeing Guild sheet means Trade and Maintain; unsigned or disagreeing Janissariat sheet means Stand down and Home. Missing or multiple asset selections take that asset’s default. Lost or disabled assets are Absent. An active Standing Commitment overrides a conflicting later assignment, including a default.

Council bell: one final written order per council, with the initials required by its current article. Spoken orders and superseded notes have no effect. The final Warrant locks here. Invalid or unauthorised orders become Hold or Restraint without a penalty merely for invalidity; a valid order lacking its required asset has the printed failure cost. Resolve both councils together.

Round-one free discussion: after seeing the result, agree provisional articles and optional Standing Commitments. Round two tests what passed. Round-two free discussion prepares the final settlement; it adds no extra operation.

Council orders and ordinary consequences

Relief — Cost: offered Wayfarer, or a triggered Grey Petrel Relief lift. Effect: if delivered, Communities +2 and one Beacon wear. Military — Cost: offered Wayfarer. Effect: delivered passage enables Campaign and wears Beacon by one; no cargo Community bonus. Shared — Cost: offered Wayfarer AND Lantern. Effect: if delivered, Communities +1, military passage and one Beacon wear.

Repair — Cost: offered Lantern. Effect: Beacon +2 before crossings. Hold — no asset and no effect. Each voyage needs prepared Beacon 2+. Without it, halt the voyage and lose Communities 1; no crossing wear. A valid unfunded Navigation order becomes Hold and costs Communities 1.

Campaign — Cost: offered Spear and delivered Military/Shared passage. Effect: Communities -1; at opening Readiness 2+ it achieves combat. Below 2, disable Spear. Without the required passage it does not leave, costs Communities 1 and leaves Spear intact. Raid — Cost: offered Spear, or its independent faction assignment. Effect: Communities -1 even if the target is absent. The target’s appearance is a fixed hidden condition. If present at opening Readiness 2+, achieve combat and lose one further Community; below 2, disable Spear with no extra Community loss.

Guard — Cost: offered Vigil not already used by a Warrant. Effect: Communities +1 and guarded status against specified physical threats. It neither raises Beacon nor permits a crossing below 2. Restraint — no asset and no effect. A valid Raid/Campaign missing offered Spear, or Guard missing unused offered Vigil, defaults to Restraint and costs Communities 1. An independently assigned Raid still resolves on its own terms.

If any successful Campaign or Raid achieves combat, Readiness +1 once this round; otherwise Readiness -1 once. Use opening Readiness for combat and Warrant checks. Food, Guard and Home do not sustain conditioning. Each round Communities lose 1 for ordinary consumption. Add all Community changes before limiting to 0–4. Prepare Beacon, cap preparation at 4, then deduct one wear for each actual crossing.

The Ascendant’s remaining powers

Imperial Warrant — Requirement: Interim or MI authority, opening Readiness 3+, offered Vigil. Effect: use Vigil first, gain Communities +1 and guarded status. If issued under imperial authority without sufficient Readiness or offered Vigil, Communities -1. Outside that authority it is advice only. A simultaneous Guard cannot reuse Vigil and fails with its ordinary cost. Missing or unsigned Warrant means No.

Grey Petrel Relief lift — Trigger: the final authorised Navigation order is Relief and Wayfarer was not offered. Effect: supply that ship requirement and spend the reserve immediately, even if Beacon later halts the voyage. It cannot supply Military or Shared passage. It can coexist with a private Trade voyage, producing two crossings.

Grey Petrel Tender cover — Trigger: round one’s Shared voyage is delivered, unguarded, and its printed crew-loss consequence occurs. Effect: prevent that Lantern loss and spend the reserve; no other protection or track bonus. It has no round-two trigger. Keep, a missing selection or an untriggered task leaves the reserve available.

Provisional articles: exercise authority before settling it

Round one begins under Interim: the Ascendant chooses both council orders; the Guild has no service duty; an independent Janissariat Raid breaks discipline and costs Communities 1. Round-two authority follows each enacted provisional article. No mid-round changes are allowed.

NG / Guild discretion: Guild delegate chooses Navigation. Duty: Lantern Maintain or funded Repair each round; otherwise Communities -1. Wayfarer may Trade. NI / imperial priority: Ascendant chooses Navigation. Duty: offer Wayfarer each round, otherwise Communities -1. NJ / joint authorisation: both Navigation delegates must agree or Hold. Duty: offer Wayfarer, otherwise Communities -1. NI/NJ service forgoes a private voyage even if the council leaves the ship idle.

MI / imperial command: Ascendant chooses Security and retains eligible Warrant power. Duty: offer Spear; otherwise Communities -1. MA / military autonomy: Janissariat Security delegate chooses; no imperial Warrant. Duty: keep Vigil Home, otherwise Communities -1. MJ / joint authorisation: both Security delegates must agree or Restraint; no imperial Warrant. Duty: offer Spear, otherwise Communities -1. Each faction suffers at most one duty penalty per round. A breach does not cancel an otherwise possible independent action.

To enact an article after round one, both relevant council delegates and the Ascendant must sign the same exact terms. Domains are independent. Otherwise that domain stays Interim. Each faction may attach one available asset assignment to one exact article: Wayfarer Council, Lantern Council/Maintain, Spear Council, or Vigil Council/Home. Both owners must approve it. An attached unsigned pledge prevents that article passing. An article can pass without a pledge.

Activated Standing Commitments bind the named assignment for round two, not the actual council order. They expire after that round. A public operational plan is a promise, not a new mechanical rule. Wider communications rights and the full empire’s constitutional disputes are outside these two instruments.

The Ceremony of Settlement

Freeze weights after round two. Guild: 2 for intact Wayfarer, 1 for living Lantern crew, 1 for Beacon at least 1. Janissariat: Readiness. Ascendant: Readiness plus unspent foresight. Readiness counts in both political weights without duplicating the army. Contracts and reserve do not add votes.

Choose one Navigation and one Military alternative as a complete package. Both Guild players must approve it for the Guild signature; both Janissariat players for the army signature. The Ascendant signs individually. Pass with at least two-thirds of ALL available leverage, rounded up, and at least two positive-weight parties. Refusals and divided factions remain in the denominator. There is no inherent veto and no transferable leverage.

Collect fresh votes whenever the package changes. The test bench allows two ballots within the Ceremony. A passing package settles both instruments, including duties on dissenters. If no package passes, provisional articles expire and both domains return to Interim. Physical outcomes remain. Other institutions have already accepted the permitted alternatives and will perform the wider Charter’s formalities: this is a scenario adaptation, not a surprise facilitator veto.

Debrief: did an event change your preferred constitution? What did each faction sacrifice? Was conflict a credible unilateral option? Did each teammate contribute? Did foresight change an action, a bargain, or only confidence? Three tokens and two possible purchases guarantee at least one remains; deliberate saving requires choosing NONE.
