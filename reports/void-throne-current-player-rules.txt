The Void Throne — weighted settlement with revised foresight
Experimental paper rules. Five players, one facilitator; two operational rounds and a Ceremony. These rules supersede conflicting v0.2 and Trial A wording. Operational costs and hidden consequences remain unchanged.

1. Foresight — revised procedure
The Void Throne — Foresight revision, 13 October 2026
This amendment replaces the timing and selection of visions in the weighted-settlement experiment. Keep THREE starting foresight tokens, one optional purchase per round, all existing asset costs and hidden consequences, provisional commitments, and weighted settlement rules.

SHOW INTENTIONS BEFORE BUYING FORESIGHT
Each five-minute faction phase now has three steps. The councils still have seven minutes and free discussion still has six.
0:00–2:00: Discuss within factions. By 2:00, each faction displays draft assignments for its named assets and up to two requested council orders. Mark existing Standing Commitments LOCKED. If teammates disagree, show both proposals. A missing draft is marked UNDECLARED, never treated as agreement or a commitment. The Ascendant displays its intended directives and Warrant, or UNDECIDED. No signatures are required on drafts.
2:00–3:00: Read the public drafts and the covers of this round's two vision cards. The covers name the decisions the vision addresses, without revealing the answer. The Ascendant chooses one card or NONE by 3:00. Pay one token before opening the selected card. A missing, multiple or late choice means NONE and costs nothing. Other players may discuss the displayed plans while the Ascendant reads.
3:00–5:00: The Ascendant may show, describe or withhold the purchased vision. Factions may revise uncommitted assignments before signing their normal order sheets. At 5:00, final faction assignments and the reserve intervention lock under their existing rules. Existing Standing Commitments cannot be revoked. Council orders and Warrant lock at the later council bell.

READ A VISION
Cost: ONE foresight token. Start with THREE; no refunds or replenishment.
Target: either of this round's two named cards. You may investigate an alternative or suspected change of plan even if nobody has proposed it. Nobody must buy a vision.
Effect: read the complete fixed conditional answer on that card. Read all its branches. You may compare your displayed plan and an alternative using the card and public rules. The facilitator explains wording, but gives no extra hidden information or whole-plan forecast.
Limit: at most one card each round. No second purchase, replacement card or refund if the information disappoints or plans change. No purchases during councils or the Ceremony. A missed window cannot be banked as another purchase later.
Public record: publish tokens spent and tokens remaining. Card choice and contents remain private unless the Ascendant discloses them. Grey Petrel remains a separate reserve.
NONE preserves one point of the Ascendant's eventual signature weight compared with buying a card, if the physical outcome is the same. It is a valid decision when the available visions do not address a live uncertainty, or when you prefer to retain power and accept the risk.

WHAT A VISION PROMISES
It shows what follows IF its printed conditions are met. It does not reveal what another player will choose, make a proposed order binding, or grant authority over either council. The hidden consequences occur whether or not anyone buys the card.
The cover's scope is complete for THAT card. It is not a certificate that the whole plan is safe: the other card, ordinary costs, duties, unavailable assets and changed orders can still matter. The facilitator does not confirm a hidden condition for free.
Use final assignments and authorised orders when resolving, not the public drafts. Ordinary defaults still apply at the real deadlines. Display final sheets beside the drafts so everyone can see changes. Unpledged assets can be reassigned until the faction bell; unilateral action remains available on its printed terms.

At council bell: final directives and Warrant on the normal sheets; existing authority and asset checks apply.
Final settlement: Ascendant weight remains Readiness + unspent foresight. With two rounds and three starting tokens, at least one token must remain; that alone is not evidence of choosing to conserve power.


2. Count the power behind each signature
Guild leverage: 2 for an intact Wayfarer, 1 for a living Lantern navigation team, and 1 for the Narrow at Beacon 1 or above. Maximum 4. No points for contracts, money, promises or an asset merely being assigned to public service.
Janissariat leverage: current Readiness, from 0 to 4.
Ascendant leverage: current Readiness PLUS unspent foresight tokens. No additional inherent veto at the Ceremony.
Readiness is counted in both political weights. This does not duplicate military units or pay for two operations with one asset. A one-point Readiness loss reduces both parties' leverage by one.
Show a public leverage record at briefing, after each foresight expenditure, after each operational resolution, and at the Ceremony. Freeze final weights from the actual post-round-two state and remaining foresight. No further foresight, operations or transfers occur at the Ceremony. Leverage cannot be donated or borrowed.

3. Ratify one complete settlement
One signature per PARTY: Guild, Janissariat, Ascendant. Both Guild players must approve the same exact package for the Guild to sign; both Janissariat players must do likewise. A divided, missing or explicitly refusing faction contributes zero support. Its full available weight remains in the denominator. The Ascendant decides its own signature. Record all five player decisions to establish these three signatures.
Pass threshold: at least TWO-THIRDS of ALL available leverage, rounded UP to a whole point, AND signatures from at least TWO parties with positive leverage. No party can ratify alone. A zero-total electorate cannot ratify. A party with zero leverage may record political approval but does not satisfy the two-party requirement.
Example starting weights: Guild 4, Janissariat 3, Ascendant 6, total 13, threshold 9. These numbers change with actual play. This is a calibration for this experiment, not established balance.
Each ballot contains one printed Navigation alternative and one printed Military alternative. There is no partial final ratification. Use fresh explicit decisions if either article changes. Signatures cannot carry to a revised package. No absent party automatically signs. A valid weighted majority may ratify despite one party's refusal. This enacts the printed powers and duties; it does not transfer ownership, compel automatic asset surrender, create resources or erase the normal breach consequences.
If the package passes, its printed articles become final, including the duties of a dissenting faction. If no package passes by the deadline, any enacted provisional articles expire and both domains revert to Interim. Physical outcomes and unspent foresight are unchanged. The Ascendant's remaining actual power is not replenished by a restored title.

4. Provisional government and temporary service
Keep Trial A: agree provisional articles after round-one resolution; each requires both relevant council representatives and the Ascendant. Each faction may voluntarily attach one available asset assignment, signed by BOTH owners, to one exact article. Unsigned attached commitments prevent that article passing. Approval and commitments are independent by domain.
The public round-two operational plan is a political promise. Activated Standing Commitments bind an assignment, not an operational order. They expire after round-two resolution. Every surviving asset returns to its owner. A Wayfarer Council commitment foregoes one trading opportunity and can deliver useful service; it neither destroys the ship nor permanently reduces Guild leverage. Lost or disabled assets remain lost or disabled under the existing consequences.
All unpledged cards retain their normal independent choices. Provisional rules do not use weighted signatures in this experiment.

5. The Ascendant's purpose
Seek a workable empire: a ratified settlement with Beacon 1+, Communities 2+ and Readiness 2+. Prefer Readiness 3+ and an intact Spear, but weigh military strength against civilian harm and the government being built.
Your legacy is a government that can sustain essential routes, protect communities and manage the army without repeated personal foresight and intervention. There is no requirement or reward for retaining an imperial article or both council powers. You may accept delegation when the actual evidence gives you confidence; you need not trust an untested promise or agree merely to produce a successful ending. Explain what gives or withholds that confidence.
Personal leverage lets you shape a dependable settlement. Spending foresight may help build that government while reducing your later bargaining power. Preserving foresight may strengthen your signature while leaving a consequence unknown.

--- RETAINED OPERATIONAL AND PROVISIONAL RULES ---

The Void Throne — provisional government trial A
Experimental addendum to v0.2. Five players, one facilitator; the same two-month crisis and 60-minute schedule.

1. Agree after the first result
Round one council cards contain operational orders only. Resolve the round under Interim. During the following six-minute free discussion, propose and sign the provisional articles. They take effect at the start of round two. The article deadline is the end of that free discussion. The three existing signatures are still required: both council delegates and the Ascendant on the same exact article and attached terms. No agreement means Interim in that domain.

2. Write the intended operation beside the article
Each proposed row identifies the exact Navigation and Military options being considered, a public round-two operational plan and any Standing Commitments. Discuss what the plan costs and whose authority changes. Navigation and Military are approved separately; no conditional signature requiring the other article to pass. The plan records political promises. Only Standing Commitments lock resources. The proper authority still chooses each actual council order in round two.

3. Offer a Standing Commitment
Cost: voluntarily lock one available named faction asset for round two before seeing that round's chosen vision.
Requirement: both members of its owning faction approve the same asset, assignment and attached article. Each faction may offer at most ONE such commitment across the two articles. No player can pledge someone else's asset.
Target: one exact proposed article, such as N-G or M-J. The commitment activates only if that article and all its attached terms pass. An article with attached unsigned commitments does not pass; do not detach promises after collecting assent. An article with no attached commitments can still pass with its required article signatures.
Effect: move the asset card beside the article. Its named assignment is fixed for round two, survives a missing or conflicting later faction sheet, and cannot be revoked after this deadline. This is an early, voluntary assignment lock, not an extra power to seize assets. All unpledged assets remain free to assign normally. No new resources are created and no operation is performed now.

Available commitments: Wayfarer Council; Lantern Maintain or Council; Spear Council; Vigil Council or Home. Trade, independent Raid and Stand down remain ordinary choices for unpledged cards; they are not service commitments. An unavailable asset cannot be pledged. Grey Petrel keeps its existing separate reserve rules and cannot be pledged on a faction commitment.

Council means offered, not automatically used. Lantern Council does not itself perform Repair. The article's ordinary duty check still applies: for example N-G needs Lantern Maintain or a funded Repair. An unmet duty has the normal printed consequence even if the asset was offered. The authorised council may depart from the public plan; this does not unlock the asset or remove any duty. No new penalty is added for breaking an unsealed political promise.

At the round-two faction bell, copy each active Standing Commitment onto the sheet first. Apply normal agreed assignments or printed defaults to the remaining cards. A later conflicting entry for the pledged card is ignored; announce the reason. No refusal or unsigned new sheet can release an already signed commitment. Other assets may still be withheld or used independently under the normal rules.

4. Review the trial at the Ceremony
Standing Commitments end after round-two resolution. Provisional articles remain pending final ratification. Final ratification uses the three-party weighted threshold in the weighted-settlement addendum. A failed settlement restores Interim without changing the physical result. Final articles do not create more Standing Commitments, refund spent resources, restore lost assets or add a third month.

Facilitator procedure
State the result of round one first. Put one player-authored proposal row where all five can read it. Give each player a chance to accept, reject or counter. Circulate every concrete counteroffer and the addressed response before asking for final signatures. If trying a replacement row, obtain fresh affected approvals. The simulation allows two candidate rows within this nominal discussion; this is a bounded sampling method, not an additional paper rule.
At the bell, check exact article approvals and the two owner signatures for every attached commitment. Record each enacted article and activated asset assignment. Unsupported proposals remain Interim. Record the trial as a separate event AFTER round-one resolution; do not rewrite the earlier state or orders.

# The Void Throne

PLAYER GUIDE | v0.2 WITH EXPERIMENTAL TRIAL A | 13 OCTOBER 2026

We have won an empire. Now we must decide what we are prepared to keep doing to hold it.

By John Keyworth, developed with Codex. Five players, one facilitator, 60 minutes. A paper game: use cards, pens and three counters. No electronic equipment is needed to play.

The situation

You meet at the Seam. Reports from the Narrow, a route serving the Vethis region, describe a failing beacon installation, a stranded field cohort and communities living on diminishing stores. Armed Conclave remnants threaten traffic. The occupation of Vethis has already lasted fourteen years. Its civilian opposition is not the remnant force.

Each round represents one month of operations before the final Ceremony. The eight-minute briefing opens this period of provisional government; it is not a literal day of instant interstellar travel. The local emergency and all named assets in this pack are new prototype scenario material.

The settlement

You negotiate the two outstanding Navigation and Military instruments of the wider Charter. For this scenario, the other institutions have accepted all six printed alternatives. The Ecclesiatum will sanctify any package ratified under the weighted procedure for the three played parties, and the remaining formalities are secured. No additional veto will appear.

# From commitment to consequence

PLAYER GUIDE | PHASES AND AUTHORITY

1. Faction discussion - five minutes

Agree both asset assignments on your faction card. Both teammates sign. Put each asset beside its selected assignment. The bell locks these choices. A council can use only assets offered to it; an imperial directive cannot seize a withheld asset. Discuss your priorities before your delegates separate.

At minute 2, display draft faction assignments and requested orders, plus draft imperial directives and Warrant. The Ascendant reads the two current-round covers and chooses one card or NONE by minute 3. Pay before seeing the answer and publish tokens remaining. The selected card stays private unless disclosed. Factions can revise unpledged assignments until the minute-5 bell. The Ascendant separately lodges Grey Petrel on one printed intervention before the faction bell. Missing intervention means Keep. An untriggered reserve returns at round end.

2. Councils - seven minutes

Navigation chooses one order. Security chooses one order. Circle the order and obtain the initials required by the authority at the start of the round. The Ascendant may send and revise written directives until the bell. The final council card is binding. Spoken instructions and superseded notes have no effect.

Round one: the Ascendant chooses both operational orders. Provisional articles are proposed and signed AFTER resolution, at the end of free discussion, using the trial addendum.

Round two: N-G gives Navigation to the Guild; N-I to the Ascendant; N-J to both delegates. M-I gives Security to the Ascendant; M-A to the Janissariat; M-J to both delegates. Interim means Ascendant. No mid-round article change is allowed.

Under joint authority, both delegates must initial. Missing, multiple or unauthorised orders default to Hold at Navigation or Restraint at Security. There is no penalty for invalidity alone. An otherwise valid order missing its required asset defaults and causes Communities -1. Committed assets cannot be reassigned after a default.

3. Free discussion - six minutes

The facilitator resolves both councils and independent assignments together, announces effects and returns the cards. After round one, use free discussion to negotiate and sign provisional articles and any Standing Commitments, then post them at its closing bell. The addendum permits voluntary early asset locks for round two. Other promises do not move assets or change completed orders.

Law and physical power

The Guild can send Wayfarer on its own commercial voyage. The Janissariat can order an independent Raid. Neither requires another player's approval. A Charter obligation can make the act a breach; it does not prevent the attempt. Each faction that breaks its printed duty causes Communities -1, at most once per round. This stacks with operational effects.

Each asset has one assignment per round. Vigil cannot serve a Warrant and a council Guard. A Grey Petrel Relief lift can operate alongside Wayfarer's private voyage: these are two ships and cause two points of beacon wear.

Defaults

An unsigned or missing Guild card means Wayfarer Trade; Lantern Maintain. An unsigned or missing Janissariat card means Spear Stand down; Vigil Home. A missing or multiple asset selection uses that asset's default. Absent applies only to lost/disabled assets; it is not a voluntary choice. Their duties can still be broken. No faction is eliminated.



# What the counters mean

PLAYER GUIDE | PUBLIC CONSEQUENCES

Track: 0-4 | Start | Meaning
Beacon | 2 | The Narrow installation: relay, surveillance and traffic-control capacity. It does not measure the health of the immaterial deep.
Communities | 3 | Local stores and confidence in the settlement.
Readiness | 3 | The Janissariat's conditioned combat power. Food and ordinary escort duty cannot restore it.

Prepare the route, then use it

First add Lantern Maintain +1 Beacon, or a funded Repair +2 Beacon. These cannot both use Lantern. Limit this prepared value to 0-4. A voyage needs prepared Beacon 2+. Below 2 a council voyage halts, gives no cargo or military passage and causes Communities -1. A private Trade simply returns without a contract. Neither causes wear.

Each council voyage that crosses causes Beacon -1. A private Trade that crosses causes a further -1, even if attacked. Check all voyages against the same prepared value. Apply wear after every voyage is checked. There are no fees, abstract capacity points or transfers between assets.

Local survival and military power

Every round: Communities -1 as stores run down. Then add all cargo, Guard, operation, breach and hidden effects. Limit Communities to 0-4 once, at the end.

If Spear actually fights through a successful Campaign or Raid, Readiness +1. Otherwise Readiness -1. Apply this once per round and limit to 0-4. Use opening Readiness for strength checks and Warrants. Routine Guard is protection and deterrence, not a conditioning exercise.

A Campaign needs delivered Military or Shared passage. It causes Communities -1 and fights successfully at opening Readiness 2+. Below 2, Spear is overmatched and disabled. Without delivered passage, the Campaign does not leave: Communities -1; no combat. Spear remains intact.

A Raid costs Communities -1 for disruption even if it misses. Whether the remnant target appears is a hidden fixed condition. If present and opening Readiness is 2+, it fights and causes a further Communities -1. If present below 2, Spear is disabled; no further Community loss. A missing target does not disable Spear.

Independent trade and physical protection

Wayfarer Trade completes one commercial contract if prepared Beacon is 3+, or if it is 2 and Vigil provides Guard/Warrant protection. At 2 without protection, attackers disable Wayfarer and the contract fails. Its crew survives. A contract is recorded on the board; it cannot be spent, gifted or converted into another asset.

A Guard or valid Warrant uses Vigil, gives Communities +1 and supplies guarded status for the round. It protects traffic and crews against specified physical threats. It does not raise Beacon or enable passage below 2. Hidden effects may also depend on the final funded orders.

Damage: a disabled asset or lost crew is unavailable for the rest of this prototype, including the ending. Surviving assets return next round. No replacement, retraining or recovery action exists in this short scenario.
