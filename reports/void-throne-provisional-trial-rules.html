Trial A · experimental addendum · not a replacement v0.2 release

The Void Throne — provisional government trial A
Experimental addendum to v0.2. Five players, one facilitator; the same two-month crisis and 60-minute schedule.

1. Agree after the first result
Round one council cards contain operational orders only. Resolve the round under Interim. During the following six-minute free discussion, propose and sign the provisional articles. They take effect at the start of round two. The article deadline is the end of that free discussion. The three existing signatures are still required: both council delegates and the Ascendant on the same exact article and attached terms. No agreement means Interim in that domain.

2. Write the intended operation beside the article
Each proposed row identifies the exact Navigation and Military options being considered, a public round-two operational plan and any Standing Commitments. Discuss what the plan costs and whose authority changes. Navigation and Military are approved separately; no conditional signature requiring the other article to pass. The plan records political promises. Only Standing Commitments lock resources. The proper authority still chooses each actual council order in round two.

3. Offer a Standing Commitment
Cost: voluntarily lock one available named faction asset for round two before seeing that round's chosen vision.
Requirement: both members of its owning faction approve the same asset, assignment and attached article. Each faction may offer at most ONE such commitment across the two articles. No player can pledge someone else's asset.
Target: one exact proposed article, such as N-G or M-J. The commitment activates only if that article and all its attached terms pass. An article with attached unsigned commitments does not pass; do not detach promises after collecting assent. An article with no attached commitments can still pass with its required article signatures.
Effect: move the asset card beside the article. Its named assignment is fixed for round two, survives a missing or conflicting later faction sheet, and cannot be revoked after this deadline. This is an early, voluntary assignment lock, not an extra power to seize assets. All unpledged assets remain free to assign normally. No new resources are created and no operation is performed now.

Available commitments: Wayfarer Council; Lantern Maintain or Council; Spear Council; Vigil Council or Home. Trade, independent Raid and Stand down remain ordinary choices for unpledged cards; they are not service commitments. An unavailable asset cannot be pledged. Grey Petrel keeps its existing separate reserve rules and cannot be pledged on a faction commitment.

Council means offered, not automatically used. Lantern Council does not itself perform Repair. The article's ordinary duty check still applies: for example N-G needs Lantern Maintain or a funded Repair. An unmet duty has the normal printed consequence even if the asset was offered. The authorised council may depart from the public plan; this does not unlock the asset or remove any duty. No new penalty is added for breaking an unsealed political promise.

At the round-two faction bell, copy each active Standing Commitment onto the sheet first. Apply normal agreed assignments or printed defaults to the remaining cards. A later conflicting entry for the pledged card is ignored; announce the reason. No refusal or unsigned new sheet can release an already signed commitment. Other assets may still be withheld or used independently under the normal rules.

4. Review the trial at the Ceremony
Standing Commitments end after round-two resolution. Provisional articles remain pending final ratification. All five players must still ratify the exact final two-article package. A failed settlement restores Interim without changing the physical result. Final articles do not create more Standing Commitments, refund spent resources, restore lost assets or add a third month.

Facilitator procedure
State the result of round one first. Put one player-authored proposal row where all five can read it. Give each player a chance to accept, reject or counter. Circulate every concrete counteroffer and the addressed response before asking for final signatures. If trying a replacement row, obtain fresh affected approvals. The simulation allows two candidate rows within this nominal discussion; this is a bounded sampling method, not an additional paper rule.
At the bell, check exact article approvals and the two owner signatures for every attached commitment. Record each enacted article and activated asset assignment. Unsupported proposals remain Interim. Record the trial as a separate event AFTER round-one resolution; do not rewrite the earlier state or orders.
