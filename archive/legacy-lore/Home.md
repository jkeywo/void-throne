Throne of Ashes
*A Megagame of Conquest, Conspiracy and Inevitable Ruin*

> The universe is very old, and it does not care about you.

The conquest is complete. The peace has not begun. Aboard the [[The Seam|Seam]], the most powerful individual in the history of the [[Setting#The Settled Dark|Settled Dark]] is attempting to turn military victory into something that deserves the name civilisation. The [[The Charter of Settlement|Charter of Settlement]] must be signed today. Everything depends on it. Everyone has a reason it should not be signed as written.
