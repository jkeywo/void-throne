## Round Structure

**Phase One — Team Councils**
Each faction meets internally. Orders are discussed, decided, and submitted to Control on the faction's Order Sheet. Payment is made on submission — currency is held by Control from the moment an order is filed.

**Phase Two — Specialty Councils**
Cross-faction policy bodies convene. Specialty council orders submitted to Control on the Specialty Order Sheet. Control simultaneously resolves all Phase One orders. Resolution is simultaneous and invisible to players until announced.

**Phase Three — Free Discussion**
Players move freely. Specialty Council results from the *previous* round are announced. Income is collected — factions retrieve Materiel, Marks, and Influence from Control at their own initiative.

**The Ceremony**
The final act. The Charter is signed, amended, or fails.

---

## Currencies

**Materiel** — Logistics, military capacity. Generated by industrial worlds and active trade routes. Primarily spent on military orders.

**Marks** — Commerce, economic capacity. Generated by trade worlds and open void lanes. Primarily spent on maintenance, repair, and Specialty council orders.

**Influence** — Political authority. Fixed income per faction per round. Primarily spent on political and legislative orders.

*All Materiel and Marks income derives from map holdings and open void lanes. Influence income is fixed per faction.*

---

## Map Control

**De jure faction** — holds the system by law, treaty, or conquest record. Changes only through political or legislative act.

**De facto control rules:**
- Faction's units present, no hostile units → that faction controls the system
- No units present → reverts to de jure faction
- Hostile units from two or more factions → system is **contested**, income does not pay out
- No de jure faction listed → **uncontrolled** by default

**Void lanes** are managed exclusively by the Ductus Guild. Only Guild units may open or close a lane. A closed lane cannot be traversed (except by Ductus military ships or units they escort). Systems whose only connection runs through a closed lane begin losing economic output each round the lane remains closed.

## Order Menu

### Team Council Orders

**MOVE**
Relocate a unit along void lanes.
- Cost: 1 Materiel per void lane traversed (maximum 3 lanes / 3 Materiel)
- Cannot move through a closed void lane without Ductus military ship escort

**SUPPRESS**
Reduce a statistic at the target system.
- Cost: 3 Materiel
- Military units only. Specify target statistic. Resolution based on unit Strength vs garrison.

**RAID**
Convert a target system's statistic value into currency.
- Cost: 3 Materiel
- Military units only. Targeted statistic is reduced; its value is paid to the raiding faction.

**REPAIR**
Remove damage from a unit.
- Cost: 3 Marks
- Any unit. Unit must be at the same system as the order-issuing faction.

**STABILISE**
Hold or recover a statistic at the current system.
- Cost: 3 Marks
- Non-military units only. Prevents natural decay for one round, or recovers a small amount.

**REDEPLOY GARRISON**
Move a Garrison unit to an adjacent system.
- Cost: 3 Materiel + 3 Marks
- Originating system's Garrison slot is vacated; its Martial Authority feed drops until replaced.

### Specialty Council Orders

**TAX**
Extract Marks from a system's Output, payable to a designated recipient.
- Cost: 3 Influence
- Commerce Council primary. Output statistic is not reduced but automatic income is redirected for one round.

**EMBARGO**
Close a system to trade, reducing its Economic Flow contribution.
- Cost: 4 Influence
- Target system's Output generates no income for one round. Must be renewed each round.

**LEGISLATE**
Modify a law, Pillar condition, or formal governance structure.
- Cost: 4 Influence
- Freeform in scope but bound by the council's domain. **Permanent effects** until reversed by another Legislate order.

### Freeform Orders
Any council. Priced at Control's discretion — generally higher than equivalent menu options. Not guaranteed to succeed.

## Combat Resolution

Combat and suppression compare the acting unit's **Strength** against the defending system's **Garrison** (also Strength 2 by default) plus any defending military units.

Janissariat unit Strength = current Readiness value.

Damage is applied to the Damage Track (4 boxes for military, 3 for non-military). A unit at 4 damage boxes is destroyed and removed.

Ductus military ships (Strength 3) may escort units through closed void lanes and protect escorted units from Raid and Suppress orders during transit.

## The Ascendant's Prescience

During each Phase One Tarot Session, the Ascendant may issue up to **3 Conditional Orders**.

**Format:** *If [trigger condition], then [action].*

- Maximum 3 held simultaneously; all can be changed or cancelled each session
- Payment held by Control; refunded if trigger condition never fires in that round
- If trigger fires, order resolves alongside other orders in the normal resolution sequence
- Invisible to other factions

## Pillar Aggregation

At the end of each round, Control aggregates statistic values across all systems upward to their Pillar and updates the board publicly.

| Statistic | Pillar |
|---|---|
| Garrison (all systems) | Martial Authority |
| Loyalty | Spiritual Cohesion |
| Faith | Spiritual Cohesion |
| Output | Economic Flow |
| Open void lane count | Void Integrity |
| Order | Administrative Order |

Pillar thresholds: drops to 1 → Crisis Event. Reaches 6 → Stability Dividend.
