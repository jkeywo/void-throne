## The Surface Goal — "The Binding"

**"The Ascendant must be Bound."**

Not killed — that is too crude, and would fracture the empire. The surface conspiracy goal is to impose a formal constitutional constraint: a council, a charter, a sacred compact, something that makes the Ascendant *first among equals* rather than sole sovereign. Every conspirator genuinely believes this framing. It gives them moral cover and a shared vocabulary.

---

> [!danger]- 🔒 Conspiracy — Cell One: The Reformers
> *Drawn from the [[Ecclesiatum Perpetua]] and [[House Vethara]]. Three players.*
>
> ### Surface Goal
> Bind the Ascendant through spiritual legitimacy — a sacred charter endorsed by the Ecclesiatum that the Ascendant cannot refuse without heresy.
>
> ### True Goal
> Restore the Ecclesiatum to primacy. Not a council — a theocracy, with the Ascendant as a sacred figurehead and the Ecclesiatum as the true governing power. The Ascendant's *name* on the throne, and nothing else.
>
> ### The Ascendant
> You do not hate the Ascendant. What you feel is closer to grief — the specific grief of watching someone capable of genuine good become the mechanism by which good is prevented. The Charter is your last clean opportunity to correct this before the correction becomes something neither you nor the Throne Eternal would prefer.
>
> ### If the Charter Fails
> Your cell has discussed what comes next. The discussion was not comfortable. The conclusion was not unanimous. You hope for the clean option. You are prepared for the necessary one. Not all of your cell is equally prepared. You know which member is least prepared, and you are watching them.
>
> ### Cell Structure
> Three members. Recognise each other by the **primary lanyard code: three horizontal bars.** One member also carries a secondary code — that person is the bridge to [[The Conspiracy#Cell Two The Architects|Cell Two]].
>
> The bridge contact believes in the same surface goal. Trust them on logistics. Do not brief them fully on the true goal until you are certain the Charter process is moving in your direction. The moment of full disclosure is a judgment call. Make it carefully.
>
> ### Goals
> - *Threshold:* Secure the Institutional Authority Clause amendment before the Ceremony. Ensure the sanctification is conditional on the amendment's inclusion.
> - *Ambition:* Achieve Charter language that places Ecclesiatum interpretive authority above Imperial authority in spiritual matters — broadly defined.
> - *Legacy:* The Binding succeeded or it failed. If it failed, what did you do next, and can you defend it?

> [!danger]- 🔒 Conspiracy — Cell Two: The Architects
> *Drawn from the [[Administratum]] and [[House Caelifen]], with possible [[Ductus Guild]] involvement. Three players.*
>
> ### Surface Goal
> Bind the Ascendant through institutional process — a formal governing council with representation for all major factions, underpinned by Administratum records and Ductus Guild assent.
>
> ### True Goal
> The council is real, but it should be controlled. Controlled by whoever holds the Administratum's archives and the Guild's navigation monopoly. A velvet oligarchy dressed as constitutional reform. Power distributed across institutions rather than concentrated in a person — more stable, more self-correcting, and more honest about what governance actually is.
>
> ### The Ascendant
> Your antagonism is practical and, you believe, compassionate. The Ascendant is a person who has been carrying something that is visibly destroying them. You are not trying to destroy the Ascendant. You are trying to build an institution that does not depend on them.
>
> ### If the Charter Fails
> The Succession Clause, if it survives to the final Charter draft, provides a legal mechanism for exactly the kind of transition your cell is working toward. The Clause is there. The person who inserted it is not in your cell, which is a complication you are managing.
>
> ### Cell Structure
> Three members. Recognise each other by the **primary lanyard code: one vertical bar bisected.** One member also carries a secondary code — that person is the bridge to Cell One.
>
> Share tactical information freely with Cell One. Share strategic intent carefully. **Do not share the council's intended composition. Do not share the Succession Clause angle.** The moment those details reach someone whose true goal is Ecclesiatum primacy, the coordination ends.
>
> ### Goals
> - *Threshold:* Secure the formal governing council in the Charter's final text, with defined authority over Imperial decisions in at least two domains.
> - *Ambition:* Ensure the council's composition and procedural rules give Administratum and Guild representation structural dominance.
> - *Legacy:* The council exists or it doesn't. If it exists, who controls it? Was that always the plan?

## The Collision

Neither cell knows the other's true goal. Each believes the other is being used. The drama is not whether the conspiracy succeeds — it is what happens when these two visions of success become mutually exclusive, ideally during the Ceremony.
