Three active campaigns at the edge of the navigable dark. Each generates [[Core Mechanics#The Readiness Track|Readiness]] when properly resourced. Each generates political consequences when news of its conduct reaches the Seam.

---

## Campaign Iron
*Suppression of the Valdric Compact · Outer Meridian · Month Three*

The [[Valdric Station|Valdric Compact]] declared independence upon the Conclave's fall — three paragraphs, no heroic language — describing six centuries of extraction and asking to be left alone. Their petition for reintegration terms received no response from the Seam.

A Janissariat force group of approximately 400,000 has maintained siege posture for three months. The Compact has not surrendered. They have also not counter-attacked. The Compact's metal production — rare transition metals essential to Guild navigation instruments and military vessel construction — has been at zero since the siege began.

The Compact's council has submitted two petitions to the Seam. The first requested reintegration terms. No response. The second requested Guild confirmation that humanitarian supply routes remain navigable. The Guild confirmed the routes are navigable. The Janissariat's interdiction makes them practically inaccessible.

**Political tension:** The Compact's position is that Charter terms should apply before they surrender — because surrendering first and negotiating after is how they spent six hundred years under the Conclave.

## Campaign Ash
*Pursuit of the Remnant Fleet of House Sorenne · Multiple Sectors · Ongoing*

The Sorenne Flag scattered the fleet rather than surrender at [[Pale Meridian]]. A Janissariat pursuit force has been conducting active engagement operations for seven months. The engagement pattern is consistent: the pursuit force locates a Sorenne element, moves to intercept, arrives at a void-position the element has already vacated.

Three confirmed engagements have occurred in seven months.

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Third Engagement
> The outcome of the third engagement is classified by A1's office. Reason: operational sensitivity. Duration: pending review.
>
> The Flag's supply chain passes through [[New Sorenne]], [[Wreck Shoal]], and [[Sorenne's Edge]]. Several members of the civilian population are independently supplying the fleet through civilian cargo networks — without coordination, without being organised as a network. The surveillance apparatus looking for a network is missing the absence of one.

**Political tension:** Every month the Flag operates, there is a Conclave loyalist in the void demonstrating that loyalty to the old order is survivable.

## Campaign Pale
*Ground Suppression on Vethis Secundus · Fourteen Years · No Resolution Timeline*

The most politically sensitive campaign. See [[Vethis Secundus]] for full system detail.

The Janissariat has established Imperial administrative control over Vethis Secundus's primary population centres. The parallel governance structure has not been eliminated — it has dispersed. The population continues non-cooperation with the specific patience of people who are not only refusing an authority but *waiting for something.*

An [[Ecclesiatum Perpetua|Ecclesiatum]] inquiry into Campaign Pale's conduct was opened eight months ago without Ascendant or Janissariat authorisation. It has produced two formal findings. The second — concerning the underground sites — has not been submitted anywhere. E4 retains it in personal custody.

**Political tension:** The Campaign Pale inquiry's findings and the constitutional status of the Janissariat's conduct are linked. The inquiry must not become a Charter issue, from J3's perspective. The Ecclesiatum believes the Military Conduct Instrument must incorporate the inquiry's findings. They cannot incorporate findings they have not received.

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Classified Sections
> Campaign Pale's operational reports contain classified sections concerning the underground sites and the three soldiers listed KIA. These are classified by A1's office pending the Ecclesiatum inquiry's conclusion.
>
> J3's private knowledge of what happened at the breach, and E4's inquiry findings, are the two halves of the same truth that no one at the Seam has yet been able to assemble.
