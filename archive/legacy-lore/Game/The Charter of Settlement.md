*Working Draft — COTS-7 (Fourth Revision)*
*Status: DISPUTED — requires resolution before execution*

---

The Charter of Settlement is the foundational governance document of the empire to be designated the **Settled Dominion**. It must be signed today. It is not finished.

The Administratum produced a working draft. The working draft satisfies almost no one completely, and several factions not at all.

---

## Part One: Imperial Authority

**Clause 1.1 — The Ascendant's Supremacy**
The Ascendant holds supreme executive, military, and judicial authority.

**Disputed:** The Ecclesiatum requires an amendment specifying that Imperial authority is exercised subject to the Throne Eternal's moral framework *as interpreted by the Ecclesiatum Perpetua*. Without this, the Ecclesiatum will not sanctify the Charter.

**Clause 1.2 — Limits of Imperial Authority**
The Ascendant's authority shall not extend to retroactive property seizure, dissolution of Signatory Institutions without unanimous Charter amendment, or unilateral revision of navigation rights.

*Inserted at the Ascendant's own direction during the second revision.*

**Clause 1.3 — Imperial Succession (The Succession Clause)**
In the event of the Ascendant's death, incapacitation, or formal abdication, an Advisory Regency Council shall govern pending designation of a successor. Composition of the Council shall be determined by the senior Administratum official present at the Seam.

**Disputed by multiple parties.** At least two Signatory parties have raised concerns in sealed correspondence. House Vethara requests precedent-based composition. The clause's origins are themselves disputed: A1 maintains it was in the original framework. A minority disputes this.

---

## Part Two: Institutional Authority

**Clause 2.1 — The Administratum**
Chartered as permanent record-keeping and administrative authority. The Administratum's Senior Archivist holds record certification and classification authority.

**Clause 2.2 — The Ecclesiatum Perpetua**
Chartered as the Settled Dominion's spiritual authority.

**Disputed:** The Ecclesiatum requires that its spiritual authority be declared independent of Imperial direction — not merely chartered as a function of the empire. Both orthodox and reformist currents are, unusually, unified on this point.

**Clause 2.3 — The Janissariat**
Chartered as the Imperial military instrument, operating under the Ascendant's direct command authority.

**Disputed:** The Janissariat High Command requests replacement language designating the Janissariat as an autonomous sacred order operating in formal alliance with the Imperial authority rather than subordinate to it. The practical difference: an army the Ascendant commands versus an army the Ascendant negotiates with.

**Clause 2.4 — The Ductus Guild**
Chartered as the Settled Dominion's licensed navigation authority, with exclusive rights to provide void-navigation services.

**Pending:** Navigation Rights Petition GUILD-14, filed fourteen years ago, has not received formal response. The Guild has indicated Charter signature is contingent on resolution. The specific terms — including the scope of the phrase *associated services* — are undefined.

---

## Part Three: Military Governance

**Clause 3.1 — The War Council**
Established as a permanent advisory body. Final authority on military decisions rests with the Ascendant.

**Disputed** (contingent on Clause 2.3 resolution).

**Clause 3.2 — Fringe Campaign Authority**
Active campaigns resourced and directed through the War Council.

**Disputed:** The Ecclesiatum requires the Military Conduct Instrument incorporate Campaign Pale inquiry findings before execution.

---

## Part Four: Noble House Provisions

**Clause 4.1 — Ancestral Property Rights**
All ancestral property rights held by Noble Houses prior to the conquest are hereby restored, subject to conditions. Disputed ownership adjudicated by the Administratum using pre-conquest legal precedent.

**Disputed:** House Vethara requests their archive designated as the primary precedent source. House Mourne requests an independent arbitration body.

**Clause 4.2 — House Military Forces**
Private household forces permitted within limits. Private military compacts between Houses or between Houses and Janissariat officers are prohibited without Imperial sanction.

**Clause 4.3 — House Representation**
Noble Houses maintain permanent representation at the Seam through the Diplomacy Council.

---

## Part Five: Economic & Void Provisions

**Clause 5.1 — Navigation Rights**
*Pending Guild petition resolution.*

**Clause 5.2 — Trade and Commerce**
Void-trade routes subject to Imperial licensing administered by the Administratum.

**Clause 5.3 — Imperial Resource Allocation**
Classified allocations permissible for up to one year.

*Note: Current classified allocations include one category designated THRESHOLD-UNSPECIFIED whose classification period has not been defined and whose operational description has not been provided to the Administratum.*

---

## Part Seven: Execution

**Clause 7.2 — Sanctification**
This Charter shall be sanctified by the Ecclesiatum Perpetua at the Ceremony. If the Ecclesiatum withholds sanctification, the Charter does not take effect.

*The Ecclesiatum therefore holds an absolute veto. This was the Ascendant's concession in the second revision. It has not been publicly acknowledged as a concession.*

**Clause 7.3 — Amendment**
Amendment requires unanimous agreement of Signatory Institutions. All amendments require Ecclesiatum sanctification.
