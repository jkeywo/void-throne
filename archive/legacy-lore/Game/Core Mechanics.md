## Round Structure

Each round runs in three phases plus the closing Ceremony:

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| **Phase One** | Team Councils — internal faction deliberation and order submission |
| **Phase Two** | Specialty Councils — cross-faction policy; Phase One orders resolve simultaneously |
| **Phase Three** | Free Discussion — deals, confrontations, conspiracy actions; income collected |
| **Ceremony** | Charter signed, amended, or fails |

## The Clarity Track
*The Ascendant's private track. Visible only to Control.*

| Clarity | Effect |
|---|---|
| 4 | Baseline — functions normally |
| 3 | Receives unsolicited impressions — Control may interrupt at any moment |
| 2 | Must make one decision per game-hour immediately, without consulting anyone |
| 1 | Sees everything; cannot act without first spending a full minute in silence |
| 0 | Control and the player determine together what happens |

Clarity degrades with each Communion and can be partially recovered by delegating decisions, resting, or performing ceremonial functions.

---

## The Readiness Track
*Janissariat faction track. Posted publicly.*

| Readiness | Effect |
|---|---|
| 5–6 | No faction can oppose the Janissariat. The Ascendant's authority is absolute. |
| 3–4 | Still formidable. Noble House forces become relevant. Dissent becomes thinkable. |
| 1–2 | Political leverage collapses. Commanders look for purpose the Ascendant isn't providing. |
| 0 | The Janissariat as a coherent force ceases to exist. |

Janissariat unit Strength = current Readiness value (0–6). Readiness degrades each round unless maintained through external deployment or internal trials.

## The Prescience System
*Ascendant only.*

During each Phase One Tarot Session, the Ascendant may issue up to **3 Conditional Orders** — standing instructions that trigger automatically when specified conditions are met.

**Format:** *If [trigger condition], then [action].*

- Maximum 3 held simultaneously; all can be changed each session
- Payment held in trust by Control; refunded if trigger never fires
- Invisible to other factions — their existence may be inferred but not confirmed
