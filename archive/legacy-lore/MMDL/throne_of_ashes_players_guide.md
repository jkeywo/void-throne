## What This Game Is

**Throne of Ashes** is a political megagame for 24-30 players. You play one member of a faction at the centre of a newly conquered empire. Your team has shared interests, private pressures, and its own view of what the empire should become.

The game is not won by collecting the most points. You are trying to achieve your faction's goals, protect your position, shape the Charter of Settlement, and decide what your faction's existence meant when the day is over.

You will spend most of the game doing four things:

- negotiating with allies, rivals and useful enemies
- submitting orders through your faction or council
- managing resources, systems and public Pillar tracks
- preparing for the final Ceremony, where the Charter is signed, amended, sanctified, or fails

Some information is public. Some belongs only to your faction or your character. Treat private information as playable leverage. You are not obliged to tell the truth, but you are responsible for what happens when people discover that you did not.

## The Settled Dark

Humanity survives across roughly two hundred inhabited worlds, stations and void-habitats. This is the **Settled Dark**.

For three thousand years it was governed by the **Conclave of Thrones**, a council of six dynastic Houses. The Conclave is gone. The Ascendant broke it over sixty years of conquest, with the Janissariat as the instrument of victory.

Now the military victory has to become a lawful settlement. The **Charter of Settlement** is the document that will define the new empire: the **Settled Dominion**. Everyone has come to the Seam to finish it. Everyone has a reason it should not be signed exactly as written.

The void between worlds is navigable only because of the **Ductus Guild**. Their navigators read the immaterial deep and make travel possible. If the lanes fail, the empire does not end today. It begins ending.

The **Throne Eternal** is the Ecclesiatum's name for the moral principle beneath their faith. It is not quite a god. It is more like a direction. The Ecclesiatum says the Ascendant is its instrument. Not everyone agrees what that means.

## Your Faction

There are ten factions at the Seam.

| Faction | Players | Public Position |
|---|:---:|---|
| **The Ascendant** | 1 | Supreme authority. Must make conquest legitimate before it curdles into succession war. |
| **Janissariat High Command** | 4 | The conditioned military force that won the war. Readiness is power, purpose and danger. |
| **Ecclesiatum Perpetua** | 4 | Spiritual authority. Holds the sanctification veto over the Charter. |
| **Administratum** | 3 | Record-keepers and administrators. Continuity made institutional. |
| **Ductus Guild** | 3 | Navigation monopoly. Without them, the Settled Dark disconnects. |
| **House Vethara** | 3 | Legitimists. Authority comes from precedent, bloodline and ceremony. |
| **House Ondrak** | 3 | Realists. Power is the only currency that cannot be counterfeited. |
| **House Caelifen** | 3 | Flowists. Trade, debt and movement are the shape of power. |
| **House Mourne** | 3 | Reckonists. Every action creates a debt; every debt is eventually settled. |
| **House Aethon** | 3 | Becomers. The self is a draft, and transformation is a political fact. |

Each faction has three tiers of goal:

| Goal Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Threshold** | A competent, achievable outcome. Get this if you can. |
| **Ambition** | A harder outcome that requires leverage, sacrifice, or help. |
| **Legacy** | A qualitative question. What did your faction become? |

Read your role brief carefully. Your private goals matter. So do the people at your table.

## The Charter

The Charter of Settlement is the legal foundation of the new empire. It must be resolved today.

The current draft is disputed in several places:

| Charter Area | Central Dispute |
|---|---|
| **Imperial Authority** | How absolute is the Ascendant's power? |
| **Institutional Authority** | Are the Ecclesiatum, Janissariat, Administratum and Ductus Guild tools of the empire, or powers in their own right? |
| **Military Governance** | Who directs campaigns, and under what moral or legal limits? |
| **Noble House Provisions** | What property and military rights survive the conquest? |
| **Economic and Void Provisions** | Who controls trade, navigation rights and classified allocations? |
| **Execution** | The Ecclesiatum must sanctify the Charter or it does not take effect. |

The Charter is not background text. It is the central object of negotiation. You may bargain over clauses, threaten refusal, offer amendments, use councils to change the legal terrain, or trade support for concessions elsewhere.

## The Round

Each round has three phases. The game ends with the Ceremony.

### Phase One - Team Councils

Meet with your faction. Decide what you want to do this round.

During this phase, you may:

- discuss plans with your own faction
- allocate faction resources
- write faction orders on your Order Sheet
- submit orders to Control

When you submit an order, pay its cost immediately. Control holds the payment from the moment the order is filed.

The Ascendant also conducts a private Tarot Session with Control during this phase and may issue Conditional Orders.

### Phase Two - Specialty Councils

Go to your assigned council. These councils are cross-faction bodies with authority over particular domains.

During this phase, you may:

- negotiate with council members from other factions
- submit Specialty Council orders
- propose political, economic, military, spiritual or void-related interventions

Control resolves all Phase One orders simultaneously during this phase. Results are not automatically public until Control announces them.

### Phase Three - Free Discussion

Move freely. Make deals. Confront people. Repair the damage you caused earlier, or pretend someone else caused it.

During this phase, you may:

- talk to any player
- collect income from Control
- respond to announced results
- negotiate support for Charter clauses, orders or final Ceremony positions

Specialty Council results from the previous round are announced in this phase unless Control tells you otherwise.

### End of Round - Pillar Aggregation

Control aggregates system statistics into the five public Pillars and updates the board.

If a Pillar drops to **1**, its Crisis Event triggers. If a Pillar reaches **6**, a Stability Dividend triggers.

## Resources

There are three currencies.

| Currency | Source | Main Uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Materiel** | Industrial worlds and active trade routes | Movement, military orders, garrison redeployment |
| **Marks** | Trade worlds and open void lanes | Repairs, maintenance, economic and council work |
| **Influence** | Fixed income by faction | Political orders, council action, legislation |

Influence income is fixed each round:

| Faction | Influence |
|---|:---:|
| The Ascendant | 8 |
| Ecclesiatum Perpetua | 6 |
| Administratum | 4 |
| Each Noble House | 3 |
| Janissariat High Command | 2 |
| Ductus Guild | 2 |

Materiel and Marks depend on map holdings and open void lanes. If a system is contested or cut off, do not assume it will keep paying.

## The Map

The map is made of **systems** connected by **void lanes**.

A system can have the following statistics:

| Statistic | What It Represents | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Garrison** | Military control and defensive force | Martial Authority |
| **Loyalty** | Political loyalty and local acceptance | Spiritual Cohesion |
| **Faith** | Religious confidence and spiritual cohesion | Spiritual Cohesion |
| **Output** | Economic production | Economic Flow |
| **Order** | Administrative function and social stability | Administrative Order |

Every system has a Garrison statistic. Major systems have several additional statistics.

### Control

Each system has a **de jure faction**, meaning the faction that holds it by law, treaty or conquest record. De jure control changes only through political or legislative action.

Actual control is determined by presence:

| Situation | Control Result |
|---|---|
| One faction has units present and no hostile units are present | That faction controls the system. |
| No units are present | The system reverts to its de jure faction. |
| Hostile units from two or more factions are present | The system is contested and pays no income. |
| No de jure faction is listed | The system is uncontrolled by default. |

### Void Lanes

Void lanes are managed exclusively by the Ductus Guild.

Only Guild units may open or close a lane. A closed lane cannot be traversed unless the moving unit is a Ductus military ship or is escorted by one.

If a system's only connection runs through a closed lane, it begins losing economic output each round the lane remains closed.

## Orders

Orders are the main way you change the game state. Write them clearly. Name the acting unit or council, the target, the cost, and the intended effect.

### MOVE

- **Cost:** 1 Materiel per void lane traversed, maximum 3 Materiel
- **Requirement:** The route must be legal. Closed lanes require Ductus escort unless the moving unit is a Ductus military ship.
- **Target:** One unit and destination system
- **Effect:** Relocate the unit along void lanes.

### SUPPRESS

- **Cost:** 3 Materiel
- **Requirement:** Military units only
- **Target:** One statistic at the target system
- **Effect:** Reduce the target statistic. Resolution compares unit Strength against the system's Garrison and defending military units.

### RAID

- **Cost:** 3 Materiel
- **Requirement:** Military units only
- **Target:** One statistic at the target system
- **Effect:** Reduce the statistic and convert its value into currency paid to the raiding faction.

### REPAIR

- **Cost:** 3 Marks
- **Requirement:** The unit must be at the same system as the order-issuing faction.
- **Target:** One damaged unit
- **Effect:** Remove damage from that unit.

### STABILISE

- **Cost:** 3 Marks
- **Requirement:** Non-military units only
- **Target:** A statistic at the unit's current system
- **Effect:** Prevent natural decay for one round, or recover a small amount as ruled by Control.

### REDEPLOY GARRISON

- **Cost:** 3 Materiel + 3 Marks
- **Requirement:** The destination must be adjacent.
- **Target:** One Garrison unit
- **Effect:** Move the Garrison to an adjacent system. The original system's Garrison slot is vacated, reducing its Martial Authority feed until replaced.

### TAX

- **Cost:** 3 Influence
- **Primary Council:** Commerce Council
- **Target:** One system's Output and one recipient
- **Effect:** Redirect Output income to the named recipient for one round. The Output statistic is not reduced.

### EMBARGO

- **Cost:** 4 Influence
- **Target:** One system
- **Effect:** The target system's Output generates no income for one round.
- **Duration:** One round. Renew it if you want it to continue.

### LEGISLATE

- **Cost:** 4 Influence
- **Target:** A law, Pillar condition or formal governance structure within the council's domain
- **Effect:** Change the rule or structure until another Legislate order reverses it.

> **Control has final say on scope.** Legislation is powerful. It is also a wonderful way to discover that your opponents can read.

### Freeform Orders

Any council may attempt a freeform order.

Freeform orders are priced by Control and usually cost more than the closest menu option. They are not guaranteed to succeed. Be precise about what you want, who is doing it, what authority you claim, and what you are willing to pay.

### Conditional Orders

The Ascendant may hold up to **3 Conditional Orders** at once.

Their format is:

> If [trigger condition], then [action].

Control holds the payment. If the trigger happens, the order resolves with the normal sequence. If it does not happen that round, the payment is refunded.

Other factions do not see these orders unless events reveal them.

## Combat and Suppression

Combat and suppression use **Strength**.

| Unit | Strength | Damage Boxes |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Standard military unit | 2 | 4 |
| Janissariat regiment | Current Readiness | 4 |
| Ductus military ship | 3 | 4 |
| Garrison | 2 | 4 |
| Priest, Auditor, Navigator, Envoy | Not a combat unit | 3 |

When a military order attacks a defended system, Control compares the acting unit's Strength against:

- the system's Garrison, normally Strength 2
- any defending military units
- relevant escorts, orders, or special conditions

Damage is marked on the unit's Damage Track. A military unit with 4 damage boxes marked is destroyed and removed. A non-military unit with 3 damage boxes marked is destroyed and removed.

Ductus military ships may escort units through closed void lanes. They can also protect escorted units from Raid and Suppress orders during transit.

## The Five Pillars

The empire's condition is tracked by five public Pillars. Each starts at **4** and ranges from **1** to **6**.

| Pillar | Primary Stewards | Feeds From |
|---|---|---|
| **Martial Authority** | Janissariat, Ascendant | Garrison |
| **Spiritual Cohesion** | Ecclesiatum | Loyalty, Faith |
| **Economic Flow** | Noble Houses, Ductus Guild | Output, open void lanes |
| **Administrative Order** | Administratum | Order |
| **Void Integrity** | Ductus Guild | Open void lane count |

At the end of each round, Control aggregates system statistics and updates the Pillars publicly.

If a Pillar drops to **1**, a Crisis Event triggers:

| Pillar | Crisis |
|---|---|
| Martial Authority | **The Long Return** |
| Spiritual Cohesion | **The Withdrawal** |
| Economic Flow | **The Stillness** |
| Administrative Order | **The Corruption** |
| Void Integrity | **The Silence** |

If a Pillar reaches **6**, Control triggers a Stability Dividend for the faction or factions that stewarded it there.

Do not ignore the Pillars. Your private goals matter, but a private victory in a collapsing empire has a certain comic narrowness to it.

## The Ceremony

The Ceremony is the final act of the game.

At the Ceremony, the Charter is signed, amended, sanctified, or fails. The precise outcome depends on:

- the final Charter text
- which factions assent
- whether the Ecclesiatum sanctifies it
- which clauses have been amended or undermined
- the state of the five Pillars
- unresolved crises
- faction goals and public legitimacy

The Ecclesiatum's sanctification matters absolutely. If the Ecclesiatum withholds sanctification, the Charter does not take effect.

Use the whole game to arrive at the Ceremony with leverage, allies, language you can live with, and enough public stability that your victory has somewhere to stand.

## Example of Play

Robin plays C2, the Financial Architect of House Caelifen. Bobby plays O2, the Trade Envoy of House Ondrak.

In Phase One, Caelifen submits a **MOVE** order to bring an Envoy to Vorth Station and pays 1 Materiel. Ondrak submits no military order there, because Bobby has privately agreed to support a commercial deal.

In Phase Two, Robin and Bobby both attend the Commerce Council. Robin proposes **TAX** on Vorth Station's Output, payable to House Caelifen, arguing that Caelifen owns the relevant trade infrastructure. Bobby objects, then offers support if the order names Ondrak as joint recipient next round.

The council submits the order and pays 3 Influence.

In Phase Three, Control announces the previous council results and makes income available. Robin collects Caelifen's income, but Vorth Station is now politically hot. Bobby has not lost anything yet. That does not mean Bobby has not been moved.

## Player Glossary

**Charter:** The constitutional settlement that defines the new empire.

**Control:** The facilitator team. Control receives orders, holds payments, resolves hidden information and updates the board.

**De facto control:** Actual control of a system based on unit presence.

**De jure faction:** The faction that legally holds a system by law, treaty or conquest record.

**Garrison:** A local defensive force. It also feeds Martial Authority.

**Influence:** Political currency used for council and legislative action.

**Marks:** Commercial currency used for repair, maintenance and economic action.

**Materiel:** Military and logistical currency used for movement and forceful action.

**Pillar:** One of the five public tracks showing the empire's stability.

**Readiness:** The Janissariat's public faction track. It determines Janissariat unit Strength.

**Sanctification:** The Ecclesiatum's act of validating the Charter. Without it, the Charter does not take effect.

**Specialty Council:** A cross-faction policy body with authority over a domain of the game.

**Void lane:** A navigable route between systems, managed by the Ductus Guild.
