Foresight you can choose

The future has a price. Ask about the decision in front of you.

Use with the weighted-settlement prototype. Start the Ascendant with three foresight tokens. These pages replace the vision procedure and worksheet. Asset effects, constitutional rules and the 60-minute schedule continue to apply.

The five-minute faction phase

TimeAction
0–2 minutesPrepare and display drafts. Each faction shows assignments for its assets and up to two requested council orders. Show both proposals if teammates disagree. Mark existing Standing Commitments LOCKED. Missing information is UNDECLARED. The Ascendant displays intended directives and Warrant, or UNDECIDED.
2–3 minutesChoose whether to look. Read the displayed plans and this round’s two public card covers. Each cover names the decisions it concerns. The Ascendant chooses one card or NONE by minute 3, paying before seeing the answer.
3–5 minutesRespond and commit. The Ascendant may disclose the vision. Factions may revise uncommitted assignments. At minute 5, sign the normal faction sheets and lodge the reserve intervention. Existing Standing Commitments remain binding.

Drafts need no signatures and grant no authority. Council orders and Warrant still lock at the end of the seven-minute council phase. Keep the drafts beside the final sheets to show what changed.

Read a vision

  • Cost: one foresight token, paid before opening the card.
  • Target: either card offered this round. You may investigate a possible alternative, even if nobody has proposed it.
  • Effect: read the complete printed conditional answer. You may show it, describe it or keep it private.
  • Limit: one purchase per round. No refund, replacement or second card. No purchases during councils or the Ceremony.

A missing, multiple or late selection means NONE and costs nothing. Publish the expenditure and tokens remaining; the selected card stays private unless disclosed. Grey Petrel is separate.

Keeping your power is a valid choice. With the same physical outcome, choosing NONE preserves one point of final Ascendant leverage compared with buying a card. There is no reward for spending every available token.

Proposals, promises and certainty

Read the conditions

A vision tells you what happens if its conditions are met. It does not tell you what players will choose. It grants no new authority and commits no asset. The printed consequences apply even if you do not buy the card.

The public cover gives the complete scope of that card. It does not certify the whole plan as safe: the other vision, ordinary costs, duties and changed orders can still matter. The facilitator can explain the card’s wording but cannot answer extra hidden questions or provide a full forecast.

After buying, compare the proposed course and an alternative using the printed branches. If plans change later, keep the same card; its conditions still apply. You do not receive a new vision.
