THE VOID THRONE / 0.2

Run the prototype

FACILITATOR ONLY | VERSION 0.2 | SPOILERS

Five players and one facilitator. Print the 15-page player pack and this pack single-sided A4 at 100%. Cut player pp. 7-12 as marked and facilitator p. 5. Grey Petrel is recorded on player p. 13; use one coin to show its availability. Bring three track counters and pens.

Briefing: eight minutes

Seat one Guild and one Janissariat delegate at each council; the Ascendant attends Security. Teammates meet together in faction phases. Explain that each round compresses one month, these are two outstanding Charter instruments, and the other institutions' consent is already arranged.

Readiness measures conditioned power, not food or virtue. Civilian opposition on Vethis is separate from the armed remnants named as targets. New asset names and the local emergency belong to this prototype. No player needs the old lore files.

Start Beacon 2, Communities 3, Readiness 3. All four faction assets are ready; Grey Petrel is unused; commercial contracts 0. Record opening article preferences. Show public consequences, including the risks of Trade, but keep the four vision cards private.

What to adjudicate

Apply only printed outcomes. Clarify terms without advising a strategy. No invented combat, negotiation bonus, prophecy, replacement asset or retroactive action. Both factions have shared objectives. Let each explain an unconventional political bargain, but it changes no printed cost.

This revision has been desk-checked. It has not had a fresh six-agent simulation or a human timing test. Resolution is more involved than v0.1; use the numbered sequence and record actual elapsed time.

Resolution sequence

FACILITATOR ONLY | USE OPENING READINESS THROUGHOUT

Record: opening Beacon/Communities/Readiness, asset condition, assignments, articles, both authorised orders, Warrant and reserve. Missing faction signatures use the player-guide defaults. Absent assets cannot be offered.

1. Authority, Warrant and Guard

Invalid, missing or unsigned council orders become Hold/Restraint, without penalty. Check Warrant first: Interim/M-I + opening Readiness 3+ + offered Vigil. Valid: guarded, Communities +1, Vigil spent. Under those articles but unsupported: Communities -1. Outside those articles: no effect. Then fund Guard with offered, unused Vigil; otherwise Restraint and Communities -1. A funded Guard gives guarded and Communities +1.

2. Fund Navigation and prepare Beacon

Costs: Relief/Military Wayfarer; Shared Wayfarer + Lantern; Repair Lantern; Hold none. A lodged Relief lift replaces missing offered Wayfarer for Relief only; spend Grey Petrel now. Otherwise an unfunded order becomes Hold and Communities -1. Add Maintain +1 or funded Repair +2 to Beacon, cap at 4. Record this prepared Beacon.

3. Resolve council passage and independent Trade

Relief/Military/Shared crosses only at prepared Beacon 2+. If halted: no cargo/passage, Communities -1, no wear. If crossed: Relief C+2; Military enables Campaign; Shared C+1 and enables Campaign. Record one wear per crossing. Check Trade against that same prepared Beacon: below 2, halt intact; at 2 unguarded, disable Wayfarer with no contract; otherwise complete one contract. Any Trade crossing, even attacked, adds one wear.

4. Check duties and military operations

Use the duty table on p. 3; each faction can lose C1 once for breaches. Fund Campaign/Raid only from offered Spear. Missing Spear: Restraint and C-1. An independent Spear Raid still resolves separately. Campaign without delivered Military/Shared passage: C-1, no fight, Spear intact. With passage: C-1, then opening Readiness 2+ means combat; below 2 disables Spear.

Raid always gives C-1. Check the round's hidden target condition (p. 4). Present at opening Readiness 2+: combat and another C-1. Present below 2: disable Spear, no further C loss. Absent: no combat, Spear intact. A council order cannot cancel an independent assignment.

5. Hidden effects, reserve and final tracks

Apply every applicable hidden row on p. 4, whether seen or not. Tender cover can prevent only the R1 crew loss. Spend it only if that trigger occurs. Return any untriggered intervention. Spent reserve never returns.

Final Beacon = prepared Beacon minus total wear, limited 0-4. Final Communities = opening value -1 consumption + all accumulated changes, limited 0-4 once. Final Readiness = opening value +1 if there was successful combat, otherwise -1, limited 0-4. Post permanent asset changes and completed contracts.

6. Announce and change authority

Explain what resolved, why it succeeded or failed, and what was lost. After R1 only, enact each correctly signed article for R2; otherwise that domain stays Interim. The new duties never apply retrospectively. Clear assignments next round; restore only surviving assets.

Hidden consequences

Any Raid: target presenceThe remnant detachment appears only if Wayfarer's private Trade completes a contract this round. Otherwise it remains concealed. Apply the public Raid strength and cost rules.

Round two

ConditionFixed consequence
Military voyage crosses without Guard/WarrantMilitary-only loading leaves local stores exposed: Communities -1. Grey Petrel Tender cover cannot prevent this.
Funded Repair + completed private TradeThe repaired relay and commercial food sellers support local distribution: Communities +1. A halted or attacked Trade does not qualify.
Any Raid: target presenceThe detachment appears only if a Relief voyage crosses this round, including a Grey Petrel Relief lift. Shared cargo does not qualify: the detachment avoids a convoy carrying the cohort. Apply the public Raid strength and cost rules.

No other hidden rules

R1 and R2 rules apply only in their named round. Target presence is evaluated after passage and Trade, not from intentions. A Raid still needs Spear and opening Readiness 2+ to produce successful combat. At Beacon 0, a Repair with Trade can still make the R2 Navigation benefit reachable; there is no uncovered middle Beacon value.

The remnant has different movements in the two months. The visions reveal operational intelligence about those movements and their consequences. They do not reveal the complete scientific mechanism of veterans' returning perceptions or certify a rehabilitation programme.

Foresight handling

Deal one chosen card at each faction phase. Let the Ascendant retain, show or quote it. Clarify the exact trigger. Never disclose the other card or suggest an order. The four cards are complete: their unseen consequences still apply. If a condition is avoided, do not invent a replacement disaster.

What has deliberately been adapted

This local scenario requires successful combat to sustain conditioning. The original larger game also allowed properly resourced deployment and internal trials. The narrower eligibility is printed as a prototype rule. It is not a claim that the old lore established that exact restriction.

Guild licences and their fourteen-year petition remain in the briefs. The deeper mysteries, veteran-training programme, succession politics and wider communications rights remain outside this two-instrument exercise. No rapid conversion of veterans into navigators is available.

Four private visions

CUT INTO FOUR | DEAL ONLY THE CHOSEN CARD

R1 NAVIGATION | The open berth

Soldiers can secure a landing place. They cannot teach a dead navigator what the deep is saying.

If Shared passage crosses without Guard or a valid Warrant: remnant saboteurs kill Lantern's navigation team at the transfer berth. Lantern is permanently unavailable.

If guarded: the attack is prevented. A lodged Grey Petrel Tender cover also prevents the loss and is spent if needed. It adds no Communities or Beacon.

If Shared does not cross: this consequence does not occur. Normal passage, wear and military rules still apply.

R1 SECURITY | The commercial lure

The remnant is watching the merchants. An army sent to an empty berth will find only its own footsteps.

If Wayfarer completes a private commercial contract: the local remnant detachment appears. Any properly assigned Raid meets it; at opening Readiness 2+ there is successful combat.

Otherwise: any Raid misses the target. It still costs Communities -1, but produces no combat and does not disable Spear.

Public limits: a present target below Readiness 2 disables Spear. Successful Raid costs Communities -2 total. Apply one Readiness +1 for combat, otherwise -1.

R2 NAVIGATION | Who gets the stores?

A ship can arrive and still leave the people waiting. A repaired signal can bring merchants to a berth they would otherwise pass.

If Military passage crosses without Guard or valid Warrant: exposed local stores are stripped during military-only loading. Communities -1 in addition to all other effects.

If Repair is funded and Wayfarer completes private Trade: commercial distribution uses the repaired relay. Communities +1 in addition to all other effects.

Otherwise: neither extra effect applies. Use actual completed voyages and funded Repair, not promises. These conditions have no opening-Beacon gap.

R2 SECURITY | The relief ships

Last month's target has learned. It will approach relief traffic, but will not approach a convoy carrying your cohort.

If a Relief voyage crosses: the remnant detachment appears. A properly assigned Raid meets it. Grey Petrel Relief lift qualifies.

If there is no delivered Relief: the Raid misses. Shared and Military passage do not draw this detachment out.

Public limits: target present + opening Readiness 2+: combat and Communities -2 total. Present below 2: Spear disabled, C-1. Absent: C-1, no combat, intact. Combat gives Readiness +1; otherwise -1.

Round two: relief and restraint

The price of that bargain

Guild: both commercial contracts forgone; Beacon finishes below its preferred 2; sole discretion surrendered.
Janissariat: one peaceful round costs conditioned power; future lawful fighting needs Guild consent; military autonomy surrendered.
Ascendant: both operational powers shared and the independent Warrant lost. The army remains capable, but is no longer available on the same terms.

This example shows survival with concessions. It does not establish an optimal or balanced strategy. The players may value different powers more highly. If they ratify another package, explain its future duties without applying another month of effects.

Edge cases and the ending

FACILITATOR ONLY | USE THESE RULINGS
CaseExact ruling
Withheld assets and directivesTrade/Maintain and Stand down/Home leave Shared and Campaign unfunded. Each order defaults with C-1. An unsupported Interim Warrant adds C-1. Private Trade may still succeed; no assets are seized.
Contradictory instructionsThe final valid council card supersedes earlier imperial notes. An invalid council order defaults without penalty for invalidity alone. A valid Warrant uses Vigil before any council Guard; a second claim fails with C-1.

With Vigil Home and no council action, C3 becomes 0: consumption -1, Raid -2, Interim breach -1. Replace private Trade with Relief and the same Raid misses.
Two peaceful supplied roundsOpening R3 falls to 2 then 1 even if Relief and Guard succeed. In round two the R2 opening value cannot support a Warrant.

Deadlock and absent cardsUse printed defaults. Broken duties still cost C-1. A faction is never eliminated, even with all its operational assets absent.
Failed ratificationAny missing player approval means neither final instrument is enacted. Both provisional articles expire. Interim returns, but no asset, track or contract is restored.

Describe the ending

Beacon: 0 closed; 1 fragile and unable to carry a voyage before work; 2 serviceable; 3-4 robust.
Communities: 0 local settlement in revolt and scarcity; 1 desperate; 2 surviving; 3-4 supplied with growing confidence.
Readiness: 0 no coherent local conditioned force; 1 weak and questioning; 2 capable but insufficient for an imperial Warrant; 3-4 strong military support, subject to the powers actually ratified.

State lost or disabled assets and unfulfilled contracts separately. Fraying is loss of conditioned combat power, not a judgement that the soldiers becoming more human is bad. No ending compels betrayal or invents successful veteran rehabilitation.

What the checks establish

The accompanying design note records the current desk checks and their limits. Before another simulation, read both packs as a set. During a human test, time resolution and ask whether each player understood their own two cards. If not, reduce exceptional effects before adding more assets.
